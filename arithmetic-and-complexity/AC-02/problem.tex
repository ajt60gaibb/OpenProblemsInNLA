\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

Determine the least integer \(r\) for which there are complex
coefficients \(u_{t,ij},v_{t,ij},w_{ij,t}\) satisfying, for every pair
of complex \(3\times3\) matrices,

\[(AB)_{ij}=\sum_{t=1}^{r} w_{ij,t}
 \left(\sum_{a,b=1}^{3}u_{t,ab}A_{ab}\right)
 \left(\sum_{c,d=1}^{3}v_{t,cd}B_{cd}\right).\]

Scalar linear combinations are free in this bilinear-rank model. The
classical upper bound is 23; deciding whether 22 products suffice is
part of determining the exact minimum. Algorithms mixing entries of both
inputs within a factor are outside this specified model.

\subsection{Why it matters}\label{why-it-matters}

Such identities can be applied recursively to matrix blocks.

\subsection{References and status}\label{references-and-status}

Bläser,
\href{https://theoryofcomputing.org/articles/gs005/gs005.pdf}{2013}, §1
and Example 5.10. Y. Sun,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27645}{\emph{An Exact 56-Addition,
Rank-23 Scheme for General 3×3 Matrix Multiplication}} (2026), gives
another 23-product scheme. C. Wang,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.07280}{\emph{Automated Lower Bounds for
Bilinear Complexity over Finite Fields}}, v10 (2026), improves a lower
bound over \(\mathbb F_2\). The later results below give a complex lower
bound of 21 and determine the rank over \(\mathbb F_2\).

\subsection{Status check --- 2026-09-10}\label{status-check-2026-09-10}

Rechecked \href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27645}{Sun's rank-23
construction} and \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21738v1}{Alman--Li,
§1}, which explicitly identifies both exact rank and border rank of the
3×3 product as unresolved. Searches for 22-product complex bilinear
algorithms found no qualifying construction or matching lower bound.
Fewer additions at rank 23 and finite-field lower bounds do not settle
this complex-field minimum.

\subsection{Literature update ---
2026-10-06}\label{literature-update-2026-10-06}

Isaac Rudich and Louis-Martin Rousseau,
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.01639v1}{\emph{Lower Bound of 22 for
3x3 Matrix Multiplication over the Integers}}, arXiv:2610.01639v1,
submitted 1 October 2026, reports in
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01639v1\#S1}{§1} that the bilinear
rank of the \(3\times3\) matrix product over \(\mathbb F_2\) is at least
22. \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2610.01639v1\#S3}{§3, displayed theorem
(1)}, \textit{recursive\_algorithm\_multiplications\_at\_least\_22},
gives the corresponding lower bound for algorithms with integer
constants that work on square matrix blocks of every size. Sections 3--4
specify this model and the reduction from integer-coefficient algorithms
to decompositions over \(\mathbb F_2\).

The authors supply a Lean formalization and describe its audit procedure
in §3. The October 6 check reviewed the paper's statements and scope.

\subsection{Literature update ---
2026-11-27}\label{literature-update-2026-11-27}

Andrey Ivanov proves that every exact bilinear algorithm for this
product over an arbitrary field \(K\) has at least 21 terms. The Lean
statement takes arbitrary \(K\)-linear forms \(a_t,b_t\) and output
matrices \(W_t\) indexed by a finite set, with
\(AB=\sum_t a_t(A)b_t(B)W_t\) for all pairs \(A,B\). Setting
\(K=\mathbb C\) gives precisely the admissible decompositions in the
problem statement. Along with the 23-product upper bound, this gives
\(21\le r\le23\), improving Bläser's lower bound of 19. Determining
which of 21, 22 and 23 is the exact complex rank remains open.
\href{https://github.com/AIV5/mmrank21/blob/cb1557b7ea80ea66fae4714628663d8c1df7a48d/Solution.lean}{Ivanov's
pinned theorem}.

The audit compared \textit{MMRank.Palomar.lower\_bound} with the
displayed problem and reviewed Palomar's successful replay of commit
\textit{cb1557b7ea80ea66fae4714628663d8c1df7a48d} on October 12,
registered October 13. The replay compared the proof with the
Mathlib-only statement using Lean \textit{v4.35.0-rc2}, with
\textit{propext}, \textit{Classical.choice} and \textit{Quot.sound} as
the only permitted axioms. Verification here consisted of inspecting the
theorem and public replay record.
\href{https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-10-13-000009&version=1}{Palomar
entry} ·
\href{https://data.palomar-registry.org/evidence/PALOMAR-2026-10-13-000009-v1/d7fb2a4a49eead03d7cf3a406c9caed9905363aac47ff064d8064eb09049e779/mechanical-report.json}{Mechanical
report}.

Tejasvi Singh Tomar establishes exact rank 23 over \(\mathbb F_2\) and
for integer coefficients by reduction modulo 2. Arbitrary complex
coefficients do not permit that reduction.
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11823v3}{Tomar, arXiv:2610.11823v3}.
