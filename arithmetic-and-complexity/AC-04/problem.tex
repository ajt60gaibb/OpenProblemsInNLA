\subsection{Negative resolution --- 24 November
2026}\label{negative-resolution-24-november-2026}

The asymptotic rank of the displayed tensor is strictly greater than
three. Its flattening rank is three and is multiplicative under the
grouped tensor powers, giving the lower bound three. Equality would
force the complex matrix multiplication exponent to be two by Theorem
1.2 of \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21738v1}{Alman and Li,
\emph{Asymptotic Rank Speedup Theorems, Revisited}}. The tensor in that
theorem is the displayed small Coppersmith--Winograd tensor with
parameter two. The November exact exponent is nine fourths, so the
equality is impossible. This update checked the reduction against the
original target and reviewed the verification record for the exponent.
The deduction settles the equality question and leaves the exact value
of the asymptotic rank undetermined.
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/arithmetic-and-complexity/AC-01/README.md\#lean-proof-and-verification-evidence}{Exponent
result and verification}.

\subsection{Context and notation}\label{context-and-notation}

Over \(\mathbb C\), the tensor rank \(R(T)\) is the minimum number of
pure tensors in an exact sum for \(T\).

\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

Let \(e_0,e_1,e_2\) be the standard basis of \(\mathbb C^3\) and set

\[T=\sum_{i=1}^{2}(e_0\otimes e_i\otimes e_i+
 e_i\otimes e_0\otimes e_i+e_i\otimes e_i\otimes e_0).\]

For a tensor \(S\), define
\(\widetilde R(S)=\lim_{k\to\infty} R(S^{\otimes k})^{1/k}\), grouping
corresponding factors when taking powers. Is \(\widetilde R(T)=3\)?

\subsection{Why it matters}\label{why-it-matters}

This explicitly studied tensor offers a route to exponent two for matrix
multiplication. It is not counted separately for other values of its
size parameter.

\subsection{Status check --- 2026-09-10}\label{status-check-2026-09-10}

Rechecked \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21738v1}{Alman--Li, abstract
and Theorems 1.2--1.3}, and searched for later small
Coppersmith--Winograd asymptotic-rank results. The paper improves the
upper bound below 3.931 and confirms that equality with three would
imply exponent two. No proof or disproof of that equality was located.
An improved upper bound or a border-rank result for one finite power is
not a solved portion of this single exact-value target.
