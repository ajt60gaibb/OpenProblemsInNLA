Abstract
For the standard unweighted rational normal curve of degree 𝑑 ≥ 2, we prove that the reduced
isotropic part of its Rayleigh–Ritz discriminant has degree 2𝑑 and its reduced nonisotropic part
has degree 6(𝑑−1). The argument identifies the critical correspondence with a binary Wronskian,
establishes the irreducibility of the nonisotropic image, and proves that every component occurs
with multiplicity one in the binary discriminant. It includes the smallest degree, the points at
infinity, and the kernel of the map from symmetric matrices to binary numerators.

8. Scope
The denominator’s simple roots are essential. The proof is for the fixed unweighted embedding and
bilinear form specified in Theorem 1, and does not silently extend to weighted embeddings with
repeated denominator roots. The degree-four numerator example is used only for 𝑑 = 2; the higher
orders are proved by interpolation and dimension arguments. No finite computational experiment
is used to justify an all-order step.
