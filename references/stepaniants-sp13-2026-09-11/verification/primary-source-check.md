- **Context:** G. Barbarino, [arXiv:1808.05555v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.05555v1), 2018. The current record still listed v1. G. Barbarino and C. Garoni, *GLT sequences and normal matrices*, Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra 41 (2025), 1-20, [primary PDF](https://journals.uwyo.edu/index.php/ela/article/download/8929/6949/23285), Theorem 3.2. Its nonnormal cases require smaller perturbations; its zero-distributed case assumes a normal sum. These contextual results are not premises of the new argument.

The proof-authoring agent read the full weak-type statement and proof and individually inspected the primary images for printed pages 12 and 23, and the original conjecture's page 29. The separate independent reviewer read the exact theorem and norm-definition text directly, verified the ordinary unnormalized matrix trace and projection-order reversal, and reconstructed all finite-dimensional and limiting steps. That reviewer did not claim visual PDF inspection. The signed reports preserve these distinct verification scopes.

Theorem 4.8 quantifies over arbitrary finite families of orthogonal projections and every trace-class input, with an absolute constant. The weak quasi-norm is the threshold-counting quasi-norm. In particular, the proof does not confuse it with a logarithmic-average ideal or require positivity of the input. Taking coordinate projections and reversing their order yields the strictly upper-triangular estimate used in the manuscript.

The published theorem credits Dodds, Dodds, de Pagter and Sukochev (1999), Theorem 1.4. The manuscript relies on the statement and proof actually read in the 2002 primary source and preserves that attribution. Neither the submission nor its independent review claims a new proof or formal verification of the external operator-algebra theory.
