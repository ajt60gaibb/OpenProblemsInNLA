## Independent mathematical reconstruction

The witness has diagonal entries 2 and off-diagonal entries 1/2, at n = 3 and α = m = 1. Thus J = (3/2)I + (1/2)11ᵀ, while S = I + 11ᵀ. The parameter condition holds at equality. All entries are positive and bounded by S; each dominance margin is 2 − 1 = 1.

The proposed inverse of J has diagonal 5/9 and off-diagonal −1/9, equivalently (2/3)I − (1/9)11ᵀ. The proposed inverse of S has diagonal 3/4 and off-diagonal −1/4. Direct multiplication gives both left and right identity products. Their absolute row sums are respectively 7/9 and 5/4, with strict gap 17/36. I independently reconstructed these products, entry bounds, dominance margins, and row sums using Python's exact rational arithmetic, without running contributor code.

Lines 73–92 compute both finite maximum row sums. The scalar comparison at lines 95–96 is exact, with explicit kernel trust. Lines 99–111 assemble the witness and compare it with the actual parameterized bound. Lines 114–118 specialize the universal conjecture to this admissible witness and obtain a contradiction.
