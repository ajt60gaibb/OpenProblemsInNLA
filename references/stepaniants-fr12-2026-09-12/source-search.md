# FR-12 source and later-work check

Checked 12 September 2026 (UTC). This is a bounded search, not a priority certification.

The coordinating and independent reviewing agents each checked the original labeled-matrix counting convention and the exact upper-bound conjecture in Ferber, Jain and Zhao, *On the number of Hadamard matrices via anti-concentration*, Combinatorics, Probability and Computing 31 (2022), 455-477, printed p.456, Conjecture 1.3. The statement agrees with the retained canonical target. [Published primary text](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0963548321000377) · [Earlier arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.07222).

The proof uses no external counting theorem. Its mathematical input is the explicit doubling operation, verified directly, and the elementary number of perfect matchings. Doubling is a classical construction; the [2007 primary doubling article](https://combinatorialpress.com/jcmcc-articles/volume-063/inequivalent-hadamard-matrices-of-order-2n-constructed-from-hadamard-matrices-of-order-n/) concerns construction and equivalence testing. The reviewed manuscript counts labeled outputs by an explicit inverse, without relying on equivalence-class sizes.

Targeted searches included the original paper title and author names with counterexample/2026; Hadamard enumeration and counting; Hadamard doubling and lower bounds; matching-indexed counts; `H(2n)`; and Hadamard counts with squared-logarithm exponents. No existing full resolution was located. Results concerning Hadamard existence, symmetric matrices, butterfly subclasses, or fast transforms were not treated as solutions to this counting bound. No claim of an exhaustive historical literature search is made.
