# KE-05 source and scope check

Prepared 12 September 2026 UTC. This is a source-reading note,
separate from the independent proof review and the public-network
eligibility audit.

The current [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.10144) lists v1
(14 July 2025) and v2 (30 May 2026), with v2 current when inspected.
The [pinned primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.10144v2)
retains Conjecture 1 in Section 3.2.

The recurrence occurs in Lemma 3 and, with the prescribed root
reordering, Theorem 1. Equation (12) defines the two separate maxima
used by KE-05. Theorem 1 and Conjecture 1 require pairwise disjoint
block spectra, without an additional ordering or noninterlacing
condition. Repeated eigenvalues within one block are permitted.
Conjecture 1 explicitly concerns independence from both kinds of
spectral gaps. Nian Shao receives the original framework and conjecture
credit.

Two targeted web searches, for the arXiv identifier with
"counterexample" and the author/topic with "conjecture" and "2026",
found no newer primary resolution. This limited search is not a
complete public-fork audit or a novelty claim.

The frozen proof is for the literal canonical question, whose exact
page is archived as canonical-statement.md. It uses interlaced
spectra and disproves tightness at fixed block dimensions, rather than
an algorithmic convergence rate. A possible ordered-block variant
would be a different question.

The proof does not invoke the source's general matrix-polynomial
product formula or its generic-nonsingularity proposition. Its scalar
first block makes the one needed product identity follow directly
from the recurrence; generic nonsingularity is reproved by rational
functions and a diagonal witness. Any separate question about product
order in a source theorem is unnecessary for this counterexample and
is excluded from the submitted mathematical claim.

No third-party PDF, image, full article, contact information, or
unrelated discussion body is included in this evidence set.
