# Entries not claimed solved

## AV-03

The general polynomial-time solution problem under the regularity promise is not resolved here. The IV-05 LP construction needs two endpoint inverses to have positive diagonal and nonpositive off-diagonal entries. General regularity does not imply those signs. In particular, the coordinate-decrease argument that enforces complementarity cannot be transferred to the general promised AVE merely by invoking uniqueness.

The bijectivity proof in IV-05 is an existence argument. Its residual-minimization proof supplies neither a polynomial iteration bound nor a polynomial conditioning guarantee. The AV-01 recognition algorithm concerns the maximum finite number of solutions for a different recognition task; it is not a solver for general P-matrix complementarity problems. No unsupported hardness classification for the promised search problem is asserted.

## IV-01

The general nonsingular sign-regular checkerboard interval property is not resolved here. The IV-03 induction is specific to principal submatrices and the inverse-M sign structure. Sign regularity constrains all minors, including nonprincipal minors whose local row and column parities need not agree with the global checkerboard pattern. The IV-03 proof therefore does not transfer to IV-01.

An optional exact search explores boundary strata of 5-by-5 sign-regular matrices and sparse checkerboard directions. Its outputs are exploratory, and absence of a counterexample is not evidence of a proof. Any generated candidate must be read and independently audited before it is treated as an additional resolution. No such additional resolution is included in the seven-claim index.
