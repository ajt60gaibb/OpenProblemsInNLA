## Dependency chain

The fixed integer matrices `S,T` define an orthogonal matrix over `Q(cuberoot(2))`. Seven fixed rational parameters and a full-rank linear system define a symmetric trace-zero quadratic form. Each column's three rational coefficient vectors are independent. The form restricts to a positive-semidefinite rank-two form on each resulting rational three-dimensional subspace, with kernel the column's irrational line.

A rational triangle in coefficient space surrounds the real algebraic column. All 189 cross-group generator pairings are strictly positive. Therefore the form is nonnegative on the cone generated by all 21 vectors, and its only nonzero zeros are the seven stated positive rays. This conclusion is proved by expanding the form on a sum of cone components; it is not inferred from sampling.

A common strictly positive rational functional proves pointedness; the orthonormal columns prove full dimension. The inequality representation is then certified by exhaustive enumeration of every six-generator subset, not by relying on numerical facet discovery. All 444 stored facets are supporting, and every possible facet must occur among those subsets.

The rational full-column-rank facet matrix embeds this cone into an orthant. Its integer Gram matrix is completely positive through the algebraic orthogonal factor. Any alternative nonnegative Gram factor has the same column space, so the rational left inverse reduces it to a decomposition of the identity inside the cone. Trace zero forces all reduced columns onto the irrational zero rays. Rational columns therefore cannot occur, at any finite width.

## Exact checks

The distributed verifier uses arbitrary-precision integers, rational fractions, the relation `alpha^3=2`, and a rational isolating interval for the positive root. Positivity is accepted only from a strictly positive rational lower endpoint. Every Bareiss division has its remainder checked. Python optimization modes that would disable assertions are rejected.

The final package reproduces the seed without numerical candidate files or third-party libraries. A separate exact symbolic construction was used during development and agreed entry for entry with the standard-library reconstruction. The facet list was also cross-checked using a separate symbolic integer-nullspace implementation before the independent standard-library exhaustive run.

The 18 unit tests include deliberate one-entry corruption of the orthogonal matrix and a local Gram matrix. Both corruptions are rejected. Unit tests cover primitives; the main verifier separately checks the complete instance.

## Specific failure modes excluded

The data do not merely obstruct a square rational factor. The proof uses no bound on width.

The signed rational matrix `R` is not misrepresented as nonnegative. The nonnegative factor is `R O`, with algebraic entries.

The certificate does not assume rational approximation preserves exact boundary identities. It proves that no exact rational factor exists.

The facet list is complete, not a subset of inequalities defining a larger cone.

The seven zero rays are proved to contain no nonzero rational vector, not merely to have irrational unit-length representatives.

A zero ray cannot be reversed inside its local cone: the coefficient-space generators all have first coordinate one.

Positive semidefiniteness of each local three-dimensional form is proved from an exact kernel and two positive leading principal minors, not from floating-point eigenvalues.

No theorem requiring a totally real field is applied to `Q(cuberoot(2))`.

These are not prerequisites for refuting the universal assertion. No priority claim or external repository update is included.
