The Deligne reference is identified through the Fouvry–Kowalski–Michel primary source; this package does not claim a separate audit of Deligne's complete paper.

## Repository procedure

[CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md), especially reporting a resolution and preserving the exact target; [Correction or resolution template](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/correction_or_resolution.md); and the [status definitions](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA#problem-status).

The prepared reports use the issue route, identify the affected entry, state scope and verification level, supply a self-contained proof, and do not request unsupported rating changes. No upload or repository write has been performed.

## Limits of the source check

Searches included the exact problem names, random Krylov/cyclic conditioning, rank-one root loci, explicit odd approximate Hadamard families, and related 2026 references. Those searches did not supply the proofs in this package. They are not an exhaustive literature review and do not support a first-discovery claim. Other entries reviewed in the same category are listed in `RESEARCH_SCOPE.md`; unsuccessful candidate investigations are not reported as resolutions.
