## What the exploratory calculations do and do not show

For SP-08, 7,680 endpoint local-ascent starts were tested across eight orders and twelve interval parameters. A separate three-block search evaluated 7,674,140 endpoint blow-up configurations across 167 order/parameter cases, using 20 block patterns up to permutation. No excess beyond numerical tolerance over the proposed rank-two value was found. These are restricted, floating-point searches, not exhaustive proofs over all admissible matrices.

For SP-09, exploratory optimizations included generic spectra and several structured configurations. Local minimizers cannot certify the original-dimension global optimum, so a numerical comparison would not itself prove an amplification counterexample. No such result is included.

The endpoint a = -1 spread argument was compared with the source and found to be an already-established case. It is not relabeled as a new solution. No special case has been given a new repository ID to inflate the count.

## Meaning of the count

There is **one proposed complete repository solution** in this round. The sign-matrix construction is a proposed complete answer to a related explicit-family question in the cited paper, but it remains **partial for IS-04**. Neither manuscript has been independently refereed or formally verified, and no priority claim is made.
