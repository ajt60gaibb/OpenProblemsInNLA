# Self-review — SP-07

The subclass inequality and its sharpness example are valid consequences of
SP-09. The important scope check is that SP-07 asks for one supremum over every
dimension and every normal pair. Both spectra may have arbitrary multiplicities
and cardinalities. No result in the pack controls that remaining supremum.

Do not promote OPEN -> PARTIAL merely from this lemma, and do not call the
constant-one statement a complete proof candidate. The stronger status would
misdescribe the exact target. Supporting proof review: `../SP-09/self_review.md`.
