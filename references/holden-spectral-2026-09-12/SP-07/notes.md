# Notes — SP-07

The useful material is the exact two-point-spectrum norm lower bound from SP-09.
It does not solve the bottleneck matching problem for arbitrary normal matrices.
For spectra with more points, local multiplicity counts do not provide all
matching subset constraints. Hausdorff distance alone ignores multiplicities.

No candidate for the global sharp constant, improved universal estimate, or
new sharpness example was obtained. Research should focus on general matching
obstructions or verified new extremal pairs rather than reusing the known
constant-one subclass. This audit made no exhaustive search over normal pairs.
