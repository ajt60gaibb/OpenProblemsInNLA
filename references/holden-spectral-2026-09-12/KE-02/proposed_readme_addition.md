## Proposed partial-result text

Explicit deterministic constructions solve two subclasses of the displayed
separation problem. For off-diagonal row-sum bound
eta<=delta/[2(n-1)], sorting the diagonal and adding a bounded arithmetic ramp
costs O(n log n) and gives gap(T+D)>=delta/(n-1). For constant-diagonal
tridiagonal matrices with equal off-diagonal magnitudes (allowing varying complex
phases), an O(n) algorithm works at every coupling strength and gives
`gap(T+D) >= delta/n^3 >= (delta/n)^3`, with `||D||_2 <= delta`.
The unrestricted n=2 case also has an explicit constant-cost construction.
