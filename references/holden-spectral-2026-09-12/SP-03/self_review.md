# Self-review — SP-03

The high-risk parts are transpose conventions and the exceptional-locus
argument. Rechecked J^T=-J, J^2=-I; surjectivity of the constraint derivative;
the dimension and uniqueness of the normal multiplier; the order of factors in
U-JUK=X(I+K^2); and the expansion R+GK+KG-KRK=QJQ.

The codimension argument uses the nonzero polynomial det(I+K^2), which equals
one at K=0. Polynomial images have closure dimension at most the domain:
on each irreducible component the dominant map to its image gives an inclusion
of coordinate rings, so transcendence degree cannot increase. Together with
the smooth dimension of Sp and the hypersurface bound in affine K-space, this
establishes a proper exceptional set, rather than merely suggesting one from
experiments. The proof does not claim the cleared quartic system is free of
extraneous denominator-zero components.

The exact rational tests check the identities for m=1,2,3; those examples do not
prove the general identities or generic count. The general identities are
proved by expansion in the manuscript. The m=1 discriminant and values at +/-1
are exact. No count for m>=2 was newly obtained.
