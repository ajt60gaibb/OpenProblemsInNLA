\begin{abstract}
We give complete proposed resolutions of repository problems MI-03, MI-04, MI-06, MI-07, MI-19, MI-21, MI-22, MI-23, MI-26, and MI-29. The first two are affirmative: the contraction constant is sharp for every summand count, and the universal positive-block operator-norm property characterizes essentially Hermitian off-diagonal blocks. Eight conjectural inequalities are disproved by explicit low-dimensional examples. For both symmetric-modulus unitary-orbit questions, no finite constant exists even in a fixed dimension. The $q$-permanent, geometric-mean norm, and determinant examples have exact rational certificates. The generalized geometric-mean log-majorization is disproved by an exact rational construction. The weighted log-majorization is disproved by a rigorous rational matrix-root certificate with explicit analytic error bounds. Separately, we identify fixed and adaptive orthogonal pinching lengths with the same sign-matrix invariant (MI-08), without computing it in every dimension, determine the sharp constant uniform over all unitarily invariant norms in dimension two for MI-09, and prove that the trace-norm endpoint constant in MI-25 is infinite. These three broader problems are not claimed fully resolved. Accompanying exact-arithmetic scripts provide reproducible verification.
\end{abstract}
