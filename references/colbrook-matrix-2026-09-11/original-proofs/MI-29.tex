\section{MI-29: an exact counterexample to the arbitrary-exponent determinant extension}
\label{sec:mi29}
The repository's extension asks whether
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mi29-target}
 \det(A^k+|AB|^p)\geq\det(A^k+|BA|^p)
\end{equation}
holds for $A>0$, invertible Hermitian $B$, and $k,p\geq0$. The established $k=2$ inequality in \cite{GAM2020} is a different, more restricted assertion; the example below does not contradict it.

\begin{theorem}
Inequality \eqref{eq:mi29-target} is false for real matrices of order three, with $k=6$ and $p=8$. One may take
\[
 A=\diag\left(2,1,\frac12\right),\qquad
 B=\frac15\begin{pmatrix}-1&2&0\\2&1&2\\0&2&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]
\end{theorem}
\begin{proof}
The matrix $A$ is positive definite, and $B$ is real symmetric with $\det B=-1/125$, so all hypotheses are met. Write $M=5B$ and
\[
 D=A^6,\qquad H=(MA^2M)^4,\qquad J=(AM^2A)^4.
\]
Since $|AB|^2=BA^2B$ and $|BA|^2=AB^2A$, the left- and right-hand sides of \eqref{eq:mi29-target} are respectively
\[
 \det(D+5^{-8}H),\qquad\det(D+5^{-8}J).
\]
In dimension three, expansion by multilinearity gives
\[
 \det(D+zK)=\det D+z\tr(\operatorname{adj}(D)K)
   +z^2\tr(\operatorname{adj}(K)D)+z^3\det K.
\]
Here $\det H=\det J$. Direct rational matrix multiplication therefore yields the following two-coefficient certificate:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mi29-certificate}
 \det(D+zJ)-\det(D+zH)
 =\frac{2089017}{16}z-\frac{31188746592549}{1024}z^2.
\end{equation}
Substituting $z=5^{-8}$ into \eqref{eq:mi29-certificate} gives
\[
 \det(A^6+|BA|^8)-\det(A^6+|AB|^8)
 =\frac{21036678407451}{156250000000000}>0,
\]
which is the reverse of the proposed inequality. In particular, the individual exact determinants are
\[
 \det(A^6+|AB|^8)=\frac{136990346414301954149}{61035156250000000000},
\]
\[
 \det(A^6+|BA|^8)=\frac{4537743716162890657}{1907348632812500000}.
\]
The verifier checks both the polynomial certificate and these values independently.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Hypothesis distinction]
The counterexample uses an indefinite $B$, which is permitted by the stated Hermitian-input extension. It does not settle a version imposing $B>0$, nor does it alter the known $k=2$ theorem. The precise target statement should be retained in any repository status update.
\end{remark}
