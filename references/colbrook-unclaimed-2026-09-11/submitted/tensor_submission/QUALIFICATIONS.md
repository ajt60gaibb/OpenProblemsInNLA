# Qualifications and claim boundaries

This archive contains candidate mathematical submissions, not a certificate of acceptance or an independently verified claim that the repository has been solved. Exact computation can check the supplied examples and identities; it does not replace the proofs of the universal statements. Novelty has not been certified by the repository maintainers or by a complete literature review.

## TR-04: pointwise strictness is not a better uniform constant

The result is a tie-aware construction intended to meet the displayed strict inequality under the conditions in its manuscript. It does not establish a dimension-only quasi-optimality factor strictly smaller than the standard worst-case factor. Its ratio can still approach that factor. This result must be submitted separately as a qualified interpretation, not counted as an unqualified resolution of a uniform-factor problem.

## TR-17: generic rank is not a bound attained by every tensor

The claim is the generic odd-order Hankel-rank target in the stated field and parameter range. It does not settle an all-Hankel-tensors maximum-rank conjecture. Nonvanishing of a constructed minor supplies a generic lower-bound certificate; exceptional tensors require separate analysis.

## TR-06: retain the hypotheses

The manifold, probability distribution, identifiability assumptions, and treatment of normalization, scaling and permutation in the proof are part of the claim. The statement must not be extended to arbitrary distributions or nonidentifiable decomposition families without a separate argument.

## TR-19 and TR-20: degree and multiplicity

A polynomial discriminant or resultant can have extra factors and multiplicities. The boundary and multiplicity arguments in the manuscripts are essential to identifying the requested degree. Small-parameter experiments alone do not establish the claimed formulas.
