# Claim status

The requested limit E theta(G_n)/sqrt(n) -> 1 is NOT proved or disproved.

Proved in the self-contained manuscript:

1. Exact fixed point u = R sqrt(v+u^2) for the complementary weighted barriers.
2. Uniqueness and deterministic global Picard convergence from zero.
3. Two-sided expectation bounds in terms of E sqrt(1+u_0^2).
4. Equivalence of the MD-02 target with the stated converged-root condition,
   provided t_n/n -> infinity.
5. Uniform all-orders first-two-iterate estimates
   n E (u_0^(1))^2 <= 2 and n E (u_0^(2))^2 <= 68.
6. A first-root Gaussian limit as t_n -> infinity.

Not proved:

- A vanishing converged-root moment at t=n^2.
- A bound uniform in an unbounded iteration count.
- A second-root Gaussian theorem or a claimed third-root obstruction from an
  earlier working checkpoint; these are not required by the current proofs
  and are not re-certified by their inclusion as provenance.
- Any substantive infinite-order subcase of the displayed theta expectation
  target.
