# Source inventory

Repository: https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/tree/main/tensor-computations

The canonical target README URLs are recorded in `retrieval.json`. Local network
retrieval failed, so this archive contains no verbatim README snapshots. The
repository targets were consulted through the browser during recovery. Their
source-reported status is not changed by this candidate package. Third-party research papers are
cited below and inside the manuscripts; their full texts are not redistributed.

1. **TR-04.** I. V. Oseledets, *Tensor-Train Decomposition*, SIAM Journal on
   Scientific Computing 33 (2011), 2295–2317.
   DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/090752286 .
   The current repository also cites the NLA workshop source:
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.05394v3 .
2. **TR-06.** C. Beltrán, P. Breiding and N. Vannieuwenhoven,
   *The average condition number of most tensor rank decomposition problems is
   infinite*, Foundations of Computational Mathematics 23 (2023), 433–491.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s10208-022-09551-1 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.05527 .
   Semialgebraic-volume background is cited in the TR-06 bibliography, including
   https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.1629 .
3. **TR-13.** J. Nie and K. Ye, *Hankel Tensor Decompositions and Ranks*, SIAM
   Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications 40 (2019).
   https://doi.org/10.1137/18M1168285 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03631 .
   Target locator: Section 6, Question 6.1 and Conjecture 6.2.
4. **TR-15.** W. Ding, L. Qi and Y. Wei, *Inheritance properties and sum-of-squares
   decomposition of Hankel tensors: theory and algorithms*, BIT Numerical
   Mathematics 57 (2017), 169–190.
   Author PDF: https://www.polyu.edu.hk/ama/staff/new/qilq/BIT-DQW.pdf .
5. **TR-20 and TR-26.** V. Borovik, H. Friedman, S. Hoşten and M. Pfeffer,
   *Numerical Algebraic Geometry for Energy Computations on Tensor Train
   Varieties*, arXiv:2512.06939v2 (2026).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.06939v2 .
   Target locators: Conjecture 3.18 (TR-20) and Conjecture 3.14 (TR-26).
6. **TR-20 auxiliary critical-point count.** F. Salizzoni, L. Sodomaco and
   J. Weigert, *Nonlinear Rayleigh quotient optimization*, arXiv:2510.17760 (2025).
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17760 ; Corollary 5.3.
7. **TR-20 intersection-theory background.** W. Fulton, *Intersection Theory*,
   second edition, Springer, 1998. Specific constructions are stated in the manuscript.

The bibliography supports attribution and checking against the named targets.
This recovery is not a new exhaustive literature search and does not certify novelty.
