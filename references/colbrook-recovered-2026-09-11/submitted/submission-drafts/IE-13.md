# Draft review note: Sharp partial-pivoting growth for unequal bandwidths

## Proposed contribution

For lower bandwidth p >= 1 and upper bandwidth q >= 0, the proposed sharp dimension-independent growth is h[p+q], with h[t]=0 for t<=0 and h[t]=1+sum(h[t-r], r=1,...,p) otherwise. The note includes a front comparison and attaining real band matrices.

## Evidence and limits

The exact checker covers 60 attaining matrices. It is not a formal verification of the universal upper bound. Fixed column order and permitted maximal-modulus pivot ties are essential.

The proof draft is `proofs/IE-13.tex`, with PDF and Markdown renditions alongside it. Run `python3 verification/run_all.py` for the six finite-certificate groups. The row-deletion notes are excluded from that PASS count.
