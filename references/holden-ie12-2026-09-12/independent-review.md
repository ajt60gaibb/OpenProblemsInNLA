## Mathematical audit

1. **Rounding and deterministic cost.** The increment/decrement floor procedure works for both signs, including exact integers, and costs `O(1+|A_ij|/h)`. Cauchy–Schwarz and `||A||_F <= sqrt(n)` give `sum |A_ij| <= n^(3/2)`, so rounding and the realized integer weight obey the claimed `O(n^2/epsilon)` bounds on every outcome. The Gaussian normalization produces an exact uniform variable through the uniform first coordinate of the two-sphere. Its zero-denominator branch is explicit and affects a null event only. The rounding errors are centered and independent, with support interval length h. The manuscript derives the needed exponential-moment bound, rather than assuming a spectral concentration oracle. The bilinear tail, 1/4-net cardinality `9^n`, factor-two norm reduction, and union bound give `2 exp((2 log 9-32)n)`, uniformly below 0.001 for n >= 1. All constants agree with `h=epsilon/(64 sqrt(n))` and `rho=epsilon/8`.

2. **Weighted-pattern multiplication.** The signed unary alphabet with terminators and padding is injective at fixed length k, including empty patterns and leading zero coefficients. It supplies at most `4^k` patterns. Enumeration, head/tail descriptors and padded identifiers cost `O(k 4^k)`. Light/heavy classification and greedy consecutive bins satisfy the adjacent-bin weight inequality; counting light runs between heavy entries gives `S <= 2W/k+n`. Encoding costs `O(Sk+n^2)=O(W)` when `k<=n`, and descriptors cover at most the original `n^2` entries. The shifted recurrence `F(j,(a,s'))=a v_j+F(j+1,s')` is valid also at the right boundary with zero padding. It uses constant arithmetic per table entry; it does not rescan each pattern. Rebuilding the complete table every product is included in `O(n4^k+W/k+n)`. Transpose preprocessing preserves W and only changes constants. Table indexing is ordinary integer-addressed scalar storage, with indices explicitly constructed by charged arithmetic; no floor, hashing, digit extraction, or uncharged matrix factorization is needed. This is consistent with the canonical entry-access scalar-arithmetic model.

3. **Kernel filter and probability.** The augmented matrix has more columns than rows, so the fixed analytic kernel direction exists without computation or rounded-matrix invertibility. On successful rounding its squared norm is at most `545/256 < 4`. Consequently `I-B^T B/4` is a positive semidefinite contraction and preserves the Gaussian kernel projection. The small-ball estimate for this projection is strictly less than 0.001; the norm-tail Markov bound is at most 0.001. Their intersection has probability greater than 0.998 and gives `||g||^2/a^2 <= 10^9 d`. Splitting eigenvalues at `eta^2/2` is sound. For the high part, the retained factor lambda and `T >= 4/eta^2` make `lambda exp(-T lambda/2)` decreasing above that threshold. The high part is bounded by `eta^2 exp(-T eta^2/4)||g||^2/2`, at most `eta^2 a^2/2`. Combining both parts proves the residual guarantee without a gap assumption and without an extra `log(1/eta)` term. Fresh Gaussian draws justify applying this argument conditional on any successful rounded Q.

4. **Original-data, A-only backward error.** The approximate augmented-kernel condition rules out u=0 on success and gives `||z||/||u|| <= (2+rho)/(1-eta)<3`. Clamping changes alpha by at most `eta||u||`, keeps its replacement nonzero even at alpha=0, and bounds output norm by `||b||/eta`. Direct substitution gives `||Ax-b||/||x|| <= rho+4eta = 5epsilon/8` for the original A and unchanged b. The displayed rank-one perturbation has exactly this minimum spectral norm. No forward-error or simultaneous A,b perturbation metric is substituted. The u=0 fallback makes every unsuccessful outcome well-defined and nonzero too.

5. **Uniform total complexity.** The comparison loops implement precisely the stated k, ell and fixed iteration count. The small-n branch satisfies `k4^k<=4n`; for n>=16, `16^k<=n` and `k<=4^k` suffice. The bounds `ell<=39k` and `T<=10000k/epsilon^2` hold uniformly, including boundaries at powers of 16. Multiplying the explicitly charged per-step cost by T yields `O((kn4^k+W+nk)/epsilon^2)=O(n^2/epsilon^3)`. Preprocessing, random draws, loop comparisons, input normalization and final conversion are included. Storage is `O(n^2)` real words. No iteration-by-iteration dense original residual is hidden in this count. Although numerical values can grow on rounding failure, their sizes do not alter exact-real operation count; finite precision and bit complexity are expressly outside scope.

The combined failure probability is strictly below 0.003. I found no missing hypothesis, mathematical correction, or unresolved case needed for the canonical theorem.
