## Proof review

- **Section 2, affine reduction:** Checked the span inequality a+b <= 10+n from Sylvester's inequality. At n=9, one span is exactly nine. Its coordinate column space equals that of E, justifying U=EC and the independent X_i. Applying a left inverse gives tr(X_i B_K)=1-1_K(i). Orthogonality of matching nonzero PSD factors gives the claimed rank restrictions. This does not establish affine reduction at n=8.
- **Lemma `lem:fiber` and Theorem `thm:binary`: PASS.** A projective rank-one curve in a linear space of dimension at most three cannot include three independent underlying vector directions: the resulting diagonal-congruence span has only three rank-one points. Thus the curve spans Sym^2 U for a two-plane, and its trace-zero part is exactly K. Reality of K makes U real and uniquely recoverable. Consequently there is at most one exceptional real attainable binary pattern when dim K=2, and none when dim K<=1. Remaining patterns inject into isolated quartic roots. The isolated-root Bezout bound is 4^3=64. The generic-linear-combination extension is valid locally at the finite set of isolated base points; a generic projective chart containing them reduces to the cited affine isolated-root theorem. Checked the source's Theorem 2.1 at https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~jan/srvart/node3.html on the review date. Positive-dimensional components at p=0 do not contribute real normalized vectors.
- **Theorem `thm:pure-count`: PASS.** The row-factor sum is positive definite by the rank-eight/nine obstruction to support in Sym^3. The stated inverse congruence and extraction map T recover independent Q_i. Rank-one factors give distinct binary subset vectors, proving the 65/70 and 64/126 bounds separately on either side.
- **Equation `eq:square36`: PASS.** The entrywise square equals 16*11^T - 7*EE^T + 2*WW^T. Constants and singleton incidence functions belong to the pair-incidence span. Independent exact modular calculation gives rank 36 for both W and the square, matching the upper bound. Rank-one columns would instead lie in a 35-dimensional homogeneous-quartic evaluation space.
- **Lemma `lem:slice`: PASS.** Subset exchanges force the off-diagonal coefficients to be additive in the indices, and the slice sums force c_ij=-(d_i+d_j)/(r-1). The stated bounds on r provide the required exchanges.
- **Theorem `thm:projection`: PASS.** Recomputed the commutator identities, which make Z_i=V_i^2-V_i/r central. On their common eigenspaces the shifted generators have scalar squares and anticommutators. A noncommuting block has even dimension. The real symmetric anticommutant of the two displayed generators in dimension four is one-dimensional, yielding at most three Clifford generators. The block count is valid, including the shared scalar direction for two two-dimensional blocks. The result is the claimed span bound five.
- **Proposition `prop:affine-lowrank`, Corollary `cor:mixed`, Proposition `prop:cover`: PASS.** Eight independent coefficients make the eight-index total positive definite. Complementary low-rank PSD sums normalize to complementary rank-two projections. The covering block is closed under that complementation; fixing b produces six independent coefficients with projection-valued three-subset sums, violating the five-dimensional bound. Thus the mixed-rank and covering conclusions follow with the stated scope.
- **Theorem `thm:quartic`: PASS by independently reproduced exact finite algebra.** Independently generated the derivative constraint matrix and integer polynomial coefficient matrix from the displayed formulas, without importing the author's builders or elimination routine. Both match the archived arrays. Integer AF=0 holds; modular ranks at the different prime 1,000,033 are 405, 90, and 81 for A, F, and the first 81 columns of F. The prime was checked by trial division. These prove the real/rational nullspace dimension and basis claim; modular arithmetic here supplies lower bounds, while AF=0 supplies the matching upper bound.
- **Section 9 and Proposition `prop:ell`: PASS.** The rank-three adjugate is a positive multiple of the opposite rank-one factor; lower ranks have zero adjugate. This gives the derivative equations and sign/rank equivalence for Q. The rank-three tangent determinant coefficient is a negative sum of three squares. In rank two, restricting the kernel determinant by a nonzero PSD trace functional gives a negative form of rank at most two; rank one gives no quadratic coefficient. For ell=0 the tangent Hessian has a scalar shift of a fixed compression of C, with its largest eigenvalue repeated at least four times. Codimension-one interlacing then forces Q to be constant on the slice, contradicting the mixed-rank result. The h*ell term changes that compression and therefore cannot be omitted to claim a full obstruction.
- **Section 10: PASS.** The rank argument through WGW^T produces the nonzero quadratic annihilator. Independently evaluated the displayed f and g: supports 55 and 56; fg=0 at all 126 subsets; all 252 covering sums positive with minimum 10. This is only a witness that the listed necessary conditions remain feasible, not PSD factors.
