## Isolated-root degree bound

Jan Verschelde. Polynomial Homotopies for dense, sparse and determinantal systems. Author-hosted exposition, Three Classes of Polynomial Systems, Theorem 2.1. Page dated 8 April 2001.
https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~jan/srvart/node3.html

Used for the isolated-root form of Bezout's bound. The manuscript separately supplies the argument for positive-dimensional fibers; those cannot be counted as isolated roots.
