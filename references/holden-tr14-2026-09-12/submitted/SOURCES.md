# Primary sources and attribution

All repository pages were read through the public website/raw website, not a GitHub connector. The access date for the sources below is September 12, 2026. Repository statuses are reports about those pages at that access, not a claim that this manuscript has changed them.

## Problem statement

**Open Problems in Numerical Linear Algebra. TR-14 — Comon's exact-rank conjecture for Hankel tensors.** The page asks whether ordinary and symmetric exact rank agree for every complex Hankel tensor, without a genericity or Vandermonde restriction. The page's own last-check date is September 10, 2026.

- https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/tree/main/tensor-computations/TR-14
- https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/main/tensor-computations/TR-14/README.md

**Jiawang Nie and Ke Ye. Hankel Tensor Decompositions and Ranks.** SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis and Applications 40 (2019), 486–516. DOI: 10.1137/18M1168285. Conjecture 6.3 is the source of the exact-rank question. The paper is background and attribution; the present exact-rank proof is given in full.

- https://doi.org/10.1137/18M1168285
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03631
- https://arxiv.org/html/1706.03631v2

## Product-space method

**Vincent Beck and Cédric Lecouvey. Additive combinatorics methods in associative algebras.** Confluentes Mathematici 9 (2017), no. 1, 3–27. DOI: 10.5802/cml.34. Lemma 4.2 supplies the linear transformation used in the manuscript; its commutative finite-dimensional specialization is reproved there. The uniformization method in the proof of their Theorem 4.1 also informs the argument. The present Frobenius nilpotent-exclusion step replaces the finiteness hypothesis that cannot simply be assumed for the ambient nonreduced algebra.

- https://cml.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/cml.34/
- https://doi.org/10.5802/cml.34
- https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.02287
- https://arxiv.org/html/1504.02287v2

## Distinct generic-case result

**Matthew J. Colbrook. TR-13 — Equality of ranks for generic odd-order Hankel tensors.** Repository manuscript, September 11, 2026. This gives the relevant compressed-Koszul context but does not supply the exceptional exact-rank result requested in TR-14. The repository characterizes the work as recovered AI-assisted research, not external human peer review or formal certification. The fixed all-moment Koszul identity in the present manuscript is written explicitly and proved independently.

## Comparison with algebra complexity

**Markus Bläser, Hendrik Mayer, and Devansh Shringi. On the Multilinear Complexity of Associative Algebras.** STACS 2023, LIPIcs 254, Article 12, 12:1–12:18. DOI: 10.4230/LIPIcs.STACS.2023.12. Theorem 19 is a comparison with generalized Alder–Strassen bounds. The present proof does not use a full-algebra lower bound as though restriction preserved it.
