# Status and rating audit: inequalities, frames and discrepancy

Audit date: **2026-09-10**. All **33** assigned canonical statements were read and checked against the primary records and targeted later-result searches described below. The audit identified a complete proof of MI-13 from a known complex commutator theorem; the precise reduction and verification scope are in its canonical page. The other 32 targets remain open on the stated bounded evidence. “Open” means retained on bounded literature evidence; “Partially resolved” exposes substantive proved subfamilies inside a displayed target, including results predating this audit. Neither is a certificate of absence of a solution. Ratings are editorial and their rationales are in the canonical pages.

Material changes: revised difficulty labels for FR-04, FR-05, FR-10, MD-05 and MD-06; narrower impact labels for MI-04, MI-13, MI-15 and FR-09. Added the published order-four Chollet result, the original Fallon–Iverson article and the SampTA theta-paper DOI. Preserved the distinctions between a withdrawn SIC claim and a solution, between arithmetic and quadratic symmetric moduli, and between permanent conjectures and false stronger variants. Published Lih–Wang theorems were checked against their broader preprint abstract.
