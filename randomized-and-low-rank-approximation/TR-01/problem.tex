\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

Let \(n\) be a power of two, \(1\le r\le n\), and \(0<\varepsilon<1/2\).
Let \(F\) be the normalized Walsh--Hadamard matrix, let \(D_1,D_2\) have
independent Rademacher diagonal entries, and let
\(S\in\mathbb R^{n\times k}\) select a uniformly random \(k\)-element
subset of coordinates, independently. Set

\[
\Omega=\sqrt{n/k}\,D_1FD_2FS.
\]

Does a universal \(C>0\) exist such that, for every fixed
\(r\)-dimensional subspace \(V\subseteq\mathbb R^n\), choosing
\(k=\min\{n,\lceil Cr/\varepsilon^2\rceil\}\) gives

\[
\Pr\left\{(1-\varepsilon)\|x\|_2^2\le
\|\Omega^Tx\|_2^2\le(1+\varepsilon)\|x\|_2^2
\text{ for every }x\in V\right\}\ge0.99?
\]

This fixes normalization, sampling without replacement, and a constant
success probability in the workshop question. Results for a single
randomization, three randomizations, or independently sampled sparse
sketches do not establish this statement.
