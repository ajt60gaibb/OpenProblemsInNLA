# RA-08 proof correspondence

This implementation proves the full frozen `ConcaveSpectralTransferConjecture` false. The mathematical counterexample is Matthew J. Colbrook's unchanged real 6-by-6 witness with `t = 1/65536`. George Stepaniants is the formalization author, Department of Computing and Mathematical Sciences, California Institute of Technology, Pasadena, California, USA. The implementation was developed with AI assistance. Agent roles are recorded separately in `verification/implementation-roles.json`; implementation contributors are not independent final proof referees.

The exact target and fourteen reference statements in `Challenge.lean` were reviewed before implementation and remain byte-identical. The generic target permits every continuous, nonnegative, nondecreasing concave function on the nonnegative half-line, including functions with `f(0) > 0`. It quantifies over every ordered orthonormal eigenbasis of both PSD matrices, including choices at repeated eigenvalues. Each function truncation uses the same chosen eigenvectors as its matrix truncation. The norm is the operator norm of the actual `Matrix.toEuclideanCLM` on Euclidean space.
