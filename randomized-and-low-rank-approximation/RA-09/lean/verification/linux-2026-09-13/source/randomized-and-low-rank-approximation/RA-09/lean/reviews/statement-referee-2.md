## Faithfulness and mathematical checks

The final contract is the unconditional affirmative assertion for every `n≥2`, `1≤k<n`, real symmetric PSD `Ahat≤A`, continuous nonnegative nondecreasing concave function on the nonnegative half-line, every permitted ordered orthonormal basis for each matrix, and every `epsilon≥0`. Its premise is exactly the **difference of squared Frobenius norms**, not a substituted residual premise. The original and transformed truncations use the same selected columns, with discarded transformed entries zero even if `f(0)>0`. No commutation, operator monotonicity, analyticity, positive definiteness, preferred eigenbasis or zero-at-zero assumption was added. This matches the [fixed canonical target](https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/5830ed4fb06da0659414a3deb2a40ad327aca052/randomized-and-low-rank-approximation/RA-09/README.md).

The real extension of a half-line function imposes no condition below zero. Every original function can be extended there, and the required CFC equality makes that choice immaterial. The actual expanded definition uses Mathlib `cfc` with real self-adjoint matrix semantics and matrix topology. Its finite-spectrum formula for a bare function is supported by the actual [Hermitian functional-calculus API](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/Analysis/Matrix/HermitianFunctionalCalculus.lean). Agreement with **every selected** eigenbasis remains a theorem obligation, not a definition or extra hypothesis.

All 17 grouped contracts were checked:

Rectangular and empty dimensions are legitimate auxiliary cases. The two-sided invariance uses actual orthogonal matrices. The relevant [Frobenius instance and formula](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/Analysis/Matrix/Normed.lean) were inspected directly.

They do not assume the transfer theorem, CFC formula or error bounds. Existence for every PSD matrix is explicitly required, preventing vacuity. The [sorted eigenvalue and eigenbasis APIs](https://github.com/leanprover-community/mathlib4/blob/0df444a360eaa60ab8c11dca51a86af692955474/Mathlib/Analysis/Matrix/Spectrum.lean) support this obligation, but their index transport must be proved. Multiplicities, arbitrary null-space choices, zero dimensions, and the `k=0`/`k=n` auxiliary tails are preserved.
- **Trace-deficit reduction:** actual PSD order is positivity of the difference, and actual PSD includes Hermitian symmetry and all real quadratic forms. The cross trace is nonnegative for PSD `B` and `A−B` without assuming they commute; a square-root/congruence or spectral argument supplies that fact. - **Admissible scalar consequences, branch certificates and full ordered scalar certificate:** actual concavity and `f(0)≥0` yield decreasing `f(x)/x` on positive arguments.
