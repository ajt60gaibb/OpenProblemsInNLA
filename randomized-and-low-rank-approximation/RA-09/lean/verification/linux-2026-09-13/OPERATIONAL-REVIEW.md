The standalone and RA-09 control suites were both checked in full. Their complete
normalized texts agree; normalization removes only invocation IDs, fresh axiom
fixture directory names, timings and resource measurements, preserving commands,
branches, error messages and outcomes.

  quotients. It rejected the raw invalid proof because a declaration had type
  True while False was required. A separate quotient mismatch passed kernel
  replay and was then rejected by the required `Quot.lift` post-check.
- Five actual Comparator fixtures built and exported both modules. The honest
  match was accepted. `simple_mismatch` failed on constant kind; both
  `simple_axiom_issue` and the fixture named `simple_kind_mismatch` actually
  failed on the illegal helper axiom. `type_mismatch` failed on the `checked`
  theorem's statement. The label is not substituted for the observed reason.
- Separate exported `sorry` and native-decision fixtures were rejected for
  `sorryAx` and `checked._native.native_decide.ax_1_1`, respectively. These were
  actual Comparator failures after both builds and exports, not fake messages
  or unrelated build failures.
- Both sandbox modes ran as UID **1001**, with private user/PID/mount/network/IPC/UTS
  namespaces, no effective capabilities and `no_new_privs`. Outside writes,
  truncations, creations and symlink escapes were denied; only the designated
  build `.lake` write succeeded. The export `.lake` remained read-only. Host
  parent access/signalling, host loopback access and AF_UNIX sockets were denied.
  Four unsupported writable-path/option invocations failed with exit two.

The nested `bwrap` executable was reached, but **UID-map setup was denied before
its inner write ran**. The observed result is rejection of that nested attempt;
