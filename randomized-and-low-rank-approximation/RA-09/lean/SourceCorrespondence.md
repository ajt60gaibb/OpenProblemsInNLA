## The canonical boundary

`ConcaveFrobeniusTransferConjecture` quantifies over every `n≥2`, `1≤k<n`, every pair of real symmetric PSD matrices `Ahat≤A`, every original admissible function, every allowed ordered orthonormal eigendecomposition of both matrices, and every `epsilon≥0`. The premise is exactly `FSq A−FSq Ahat_k ≤ (1+epsilon) FSq(A−A_k)`. It is not replaced by the source's weaker ordinary residual premise. The conclusion is the original relative squared Frobenius error with the same epsilon.

`AdmissibleFunction` uses a real extension of the half-line function, with continuity, concavity, monotonicity and nonnegativity required only on `[0,∞)`. Independence of the extension below zero is a separate genuine CFC theorem. There is no `f(0)=0`, global continuity, analyticity, strict monotonicity, commutativity or operator-monotonicity premise. The original and transformed truncations share the same selected eigenvectors. Discarded entries of the transformed truncation are zero even when `f(0)>0`; the object is not `f(Ahat_k)`.

## Computation and primary APIs

This is pure exact, dimension-independent algebra and finite spectral analysis. The rational identity `Q(d,z)=2(d−1)(z−1/2)²+(d−1)/2+4(z−3/8)²+7/16` is an adaptation by this formalization, not a displayed formula falsely attributed to the manuscript. No bounded numerical interval, sampled scalar lemma, root search or decorative singleton is planned. LeanCert's explicit kernel trust assertions and actual dependency inspection are the appropriate approved use here; every final proof must have only the standard three axioms or a subset.
