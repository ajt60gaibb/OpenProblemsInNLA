\textbf{Rating rationale:} Challenging because arbitrary support
intersections defeat existing sparse-matrix estimates; community impact
is understanding limits of sparse sketches and column selection.

\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

Fix an integer \(s\ge2\) and \(C\ge1\). Suppose \(r_k\le k\),
\(k/r_k\to C\), and \(n_k/r_k\to\infty\). For every deterministic
sequence \(M_k\in\{-1,0,1\}^{k\times n_k}\) with exactly \(s\) nonzero
entries per column, and a uniformly random \(r_k\)-element column set
\(I_k\), prove or disprove

\[
\forall\eta>0,\qquad
\Pr\{\sigma_{\min}((M_k)_{:I_k})>\eta\}\longrightarrow0.
\]

Here \(\sigma_{\min}(B)=\inf_{\|x\|_2=1}\|Bx\|_2\). There is no
hypothesis controlling intersections of column supports.

\subsection{Reference}\label{reference}

Han Huang, Mark Rudelson, and Konstantin Tikhomirov,
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05384v2}{\emph{Well-Invertible Column
Subsets of Sparse Matrices Are Rare}}, §7, Conjecture 7.1; compare
Theorem 1.3 for the additional structural hypothesis in the proved
result.

\subsection{Status check}\label{status-check}

Searches included
\textit{"unrestricted deterministic sparse sketches" conjecture} and
the exact paper title with \textit{2026}. No subsequent resolution was
located. The paper's disproof of a particular SparseStack conjecture
does not establish its own unrestricted deterministic conjecture.

\subsection{Audit --- 2026-09-10}\label{audit-2026-09-10}

Rechecked
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2607.05384v2}{Huang--Rudelson--Tikhomirov,
Theorem 1.3 and Conjecture 7.1}: a structural subclass is proved, while
arbitrary deterministic supports remain conjectural. Title and
sparse-subset follow-up searches found no full resolution.
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2607.23017v1}{Tikhomirov's later embedding
paper} studies random models and spectral-norm estimates, not all
deterministic support patterns.
