## Proved results

- `pureTensor_pivot_identity`: every pure tensor satisfies every degree-d pivot identity, including zero-valued pivots.
- `rankAtMostOne_iff_pivot_equations`: the actual predicate zero-or-nonzero-rank-one is equivalent to the complete pivot equation family.
- `rankAtMostOne_iff_eval_pivotPolynomial_eq_zero`: this same predicate is the common zero locus of the genuine finite MvPolynomial family.
- `range_pureTensor_eq_rankAtMostOne`: raw factor parametrization attains exactly that zero-inclusive cone.
- `isClosed_rankAtMostOne`: the cone is topologically closed.
- `isClosed_closedRankOneProduct`: the complete ordered cone product, including all zero entries, is closed.
- `isClosed_closedAdditionFiber`: the entire addition fiber over any tensor is closed.

Three additional public supporting theorems are checked: `prod_prod_diagonal_identity`, `reconstruction_of_pivot_equations`, and `rankOne_reconstruction_rlike`. The normalized factor definition is `rankOneNormalizedFactors`.

## Logic

The finite-product identity is proved for an arbitrary commutative monoid and finite decidable index type. It commutes a double product, separates one diagonal term per factor, and counts the remaining entries as cardinality minus one. There is no cancellation or division, so the identity applies directly to the raw factor polynomial ring as well as fields. Instantiating it with factor entries proves every pure-tensor pivot equation, regardless of whether a pivot is zero.

For the converse, zero is handled separately. A nonzero actual tensor supplies an actual nonzero coordinate pivot. Its normalized coordinate slices satisfy exact tensor reconstruction by dividing only at that proven nonzero pivot. The amplitude is absorbed into mode zero, whose index exists because d>0; this constructs the actual pure-factor witness required by frozen RankOne. A separate lemma records reconstruction for a tensor already known to be rank one. Zero itself is attained by all-zero factors when d>0.

The concrete polynomial evaluation simplifies to the scalar equation exactly. Continuity of polynomial evaluation composed with actual tensor coordinates makes each equation's zero set closed; their intersection is the proved cone. Coordinate preimages give the closed product, and intersection with the finite-sum equation gives the closed fiber. Empty tensor-coordinate types and empty summand indices are handled by the same proof terms. d=0 is excluded exactly as reviewed.

No statement says the raw ideal generated by the degree-d equations is prime, radical, or the full Segre ideal. The result is a point-set zero-locus equality. The generic CommMonoid identity can prove relations inside the separate actual generated coordinate subalgebra, whose domain structure must come from its embedding in the raw polynomial domain. This module alone proves no character bijection, coordinate-ring irreducibility theorem, generic finite-fiber certificate, derivative regularity, real full-measure locus, finite volume, or angular expectation.
