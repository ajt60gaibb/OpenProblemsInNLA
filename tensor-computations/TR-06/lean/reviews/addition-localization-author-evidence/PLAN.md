# Pre-proof actual tensor addition localization assembly

The base is the actual image subalgebra of the reviewed addition pullback inside the integral Segre coordinate algebra. It is not the full ambient tensor polynomial ring, so its inclusion in the source is injective. The source and image are domains; source finite type over C implies finite type over the image subalgebra by the scalar tower.

Assume d>0 and one actual complex tensor A of exact rank r with identifiable decomposition. Its entire ordered closed-product addition fiber is finite and nonempty by the reviewed zero-removal/permutation theorem. The reviewed character-to-actual-tuple correspondence supplies a source character f at one such decomposition. Restrict f to the image subalgebra to obtain the base character χ.

The actual character fiber over χ is exactly source characters whose extracted tensor tuple sums to A: equality on the image subalgebra gives equality on the sum-coordinate generators; conversely equality on those generators and polynomial evaluation gives equality on every element of the image. For finiteness, one direction plus injectivity of characterTuple suffices. The selected f proves nonemptiness.

Apply the separately reviewed finite-character-fiber theorem to obtain genuine QuasiFiniteAt at the source zero prime, then generic finite localization using the actual image-base inclusion. The resulting denominator belongs to the image algebra and is nonzero there, and the conclusion uses the canonical localized module action. The target accepts a witness of actual exact rank/identifiability, not an extra final analytic hypothesis. The frozen generic-identifiability hypothesis supplies such a witness directly, but polynomial/generic witness conversion may be an additional assembly lemma.

The result includes arbitrary mode sizes and all r including zero, since d>0 is sufficient for the full character correspondence. It does not yet extract an ambient polynomial, prove finite good fibers force exact rank, prove properness on real compact sets, injective analytic derivatives, smooth regularity, or finite volume/mean. These remain explicit additional steps.
