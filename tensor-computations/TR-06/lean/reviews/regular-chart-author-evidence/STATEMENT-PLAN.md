## Exact hypotheses and conclusions

It includes zero entries and all possible branches; it does not posit compactness merely for the selected pivot chart. `IdentifiableAdditionOn U` asserts exact rank r and uniqueness for each point of U possessing an actual length-r decomposition.

`AdditionFiberNeighborhoodStatement` requires open U, this complete compact-preimage property, a point A in U of actual exact rank r, and an open V in the WHOLE OrderedRankOne product containing the full actual fiber over A. It concludes that every ordered rank-one tuple with sum sufficiently close to A belongs to V. No identifiability or d>0 is necessary for this step. This is a no-escape lemma, not an assumption that addition is locally injective or has open image.

`SmoothChartOfProperImmersionStatement` additionally uses d>0, actual identifiable addition throughout U, a chosen genuine pivot pattern q0 and parameter z with every amplitude nonzero, sum in U, and injectivity of the derivative of the actual pivotSum at z. These mandatory chart fields are not separate premises. Injectivity at z alone suffices because C1 derivative injectivity is open in the finite-dimensional domain.

## Proof route for review

For the no-escape result choose a small closed tensor ball about A contained in U, whose complete closed-product preimage is compact. If tuples outside V have sums approaching A, use a convergent subsequence in that compact set. Its limit is still in the full closed product and sums to A. The already proved exact-rank zero-removal lemma implies every limit summand is nonzero, so it is an OrderedRankOne point in the full fiber. Openness of V in that subtype gives the contradiction. A compact-image separation proof using an ambient open extension of V can avoid explicit sequences and has the same content.

For the chart result choose a continuous linear left inverse L of the injective derivative of pivotSum. Compose the sum map with L. Its derivative at z is identity, so the pinned C-infinity inverse function theorem gives a local open partial homeomorphism in the full OrderedPivotData space. It follows that the original sum is an embedding of a small parameter patch, with continuous inverse supplied by the square inverse composed with L. Shrink within nonzero amplitudes, the preimage of U, and the open set where the sum derivative remains injective. Decode the patch into the WHOLE OrderedRankOne product using the existing actual pivot homeomorphism.

The remaining target-openness argument must be explicit: take the finite union of permutation translates of this decoded open patch. Actual identifiability at the base tensor makes that union contain its full fiber. The no-escape lemma implies every nearby decomposition lies in one of these translates, so every nearby identifiable tensor lies in the original patch image because permutations preserve sums. More generally the same argument applies at each point of the patch image after shrinking a local parameter neighborhood; hence the full patch image is relatively open in S. An alternative is to intersect the image with a sufficiently small ambient ball and restrict the parameter source to its preimage. The pointwise no-escape statement and local inverse establish actual open target and continuity of its inverse, not merely a bijection with an immersed image.

Use `finrank_orderedPivotData` to transport parameter coordinates by a continuous linear equivalence from EuclideanSpace R (Fin expectedDimension). Raw pivot coordinates have an ordinary product norm, so this transport is not claimed isometric and is not used as an induced-volume equality. Smooth summands come from the polynomial decoder; smooth normalizedTuple follows from each summand being nonzero on the source. The chart's total function outside the open source may use the known base identifiable tensor; all smoothness and differential statements are local on the source. The specified branch equality at the base point fixes the ordering.

## Boundary and edge cases

No nonempty/rank lower bound is introduced. r=0 has expectedDimension0 and the single empty decomposition; the IFT and exact statement should handle that case. If mode sizes make actual nonzero tensors impossible and r>0, the supplied nonzero pivot-parameter hypothesis cannot be met; no existence is invented. d>0 is required only to absorb scalar amplitude into an actual tensor mode in the decoder. Repeated equal summands cause no extra stabilizer assumption: if their presence conflicts with the injective derivative, the premise already rules them out; finite permutation orbit coverage itself does not need a free action. No global rank equality, choice of a preferred decomposition, generic fiber scheme claim, or local-to-global volume inference is used.
