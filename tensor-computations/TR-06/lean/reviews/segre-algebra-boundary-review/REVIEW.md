The source is its unital complex subalgebra generated by that finite family. Domain structure is therefore inherited from an actual domain, and finite type follows from actual finite generators; neither is postulated as a tensor-space property or obtained from an unproved ideal identification.

The character tuple simply evaluates those coordinate generators. It does not build membership in the closed-rank-one product into the map. The proposed full range equality therefore requires proving every character's extracted tuple satisfies the actual rank-one-or-zero predicate, and proving every such actual tuple is attained. The cone polynomial identities and factor witnesses provide valid routes in the two directions. Equality on all coordinate generators determines characters of the generated algebra, so injectivity is exact and not weakened to a quotient of already identified characters.
