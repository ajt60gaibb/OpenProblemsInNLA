# Segre coordinate-algebra proposal before proof

Define the complex source coordinate ring as the subalgebra of the raw factor polynomial ring generated by actual summand coordinate products. Its being a domain is inherited from the containing polynomial ring; finite type follows from the finite generator family. No claim equates this algebra with a quotient by a nonradical degree-d equation ideal. Raw factor dimension is irrelevant here: no volume or tangent dimension conclusion is asserted.

Each complex character evaluates generators to an ordered tensor tuple. The degree-d cone equations are polynomial identities in the factor entries, so their values under any character vanish; the separately proposed point-set cone characterization places the extracted tuple in the full product of closed rank-one cones. Conversely, for any tuple in that closed product and d>0, choose actual factors for nonzero rank-one entries and zero factors for zero entries, evaluate the raw polynomial ring, and restrict the evaluation to the coordinate subalgebra. This realizes every tuple. Characters agreeing on generators agree on the generated algebra, so extraction is injective. Evaluation of coordinate sums gives exact compatibility with the addition algebra homomorphism.

The proposition definitions request only these exact algebraic structure and point-correspondence results. They include all ranks including r=0 and all format dimensions (possibly zero mode sizes) with d>0 for the point correspondence. They do not assert quasi-finiteness, closed scheme-fiber finiteness from character finiteness, generic localization, smoothness, analytic derivative rank, properness, or any final TR-06 target. Independent review must precede implementation.
