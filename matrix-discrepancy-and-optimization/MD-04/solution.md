# MD-04: Application of the Guo–Fang–Lu Beck–Fiala bound

**Application-note author:** George Stepaniants.  
**Affiliation:** Department of Computing and Mathematical Sciences, California Institute of Technology.  
**Date:** 11 September 2026.

The shared [complete note, Theorem 2](../../references/stepaniants-2026-09-11/manuscripts/md03_md04_proofs.tex) ([PDF](../../references/stepaniants-2026-09-11/manuscripts/md03_md04_proofs.pdf)) applies **Guo, Fang and Lu, Corollary 1.2, p. 2**, [arXiv:2609.11189v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11189v1), to the [original incidence-matrix statement](README.md).

For a $0$–$1$ column with at most $t$ nonzeros, the corresponding column of $A/\sqrt t$ has squared Euclidean norm at most one. The Komlós theorem therefore gives a signing with $\|A\varepsilon\|_\infty<3\sqrt{2\pi t}$. This proves every allowed dimension and sparsity, with a sign for every column.

Both the original theorem and its Beck–Fiala corollary are credited to **Shengtao Guo, Ethan X. Fang and Junwei Lu**. Stepaniants authors the application note.
