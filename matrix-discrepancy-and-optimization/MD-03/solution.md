# MD-03: Application of the Guo–Fang–Lu Komlós bound

**Application-note author:** George Stepaniants.  
**Affiliation:** Department of Computing and Mathematical Sciences, California Institute of Technology.  
**Date:** 11 September 2026.

The shared [complete note, Theorem 1](../../references/stepaniants-2026-09-11/manuscripts/md03_md04_proofs.tex) ([PDF](../../references/stepaniants-2026-09-11/manuscripts/md03_md04_proofs.pdf)) applies **Guo, Fang and Lu, Theorem 1.1, p. 1**, [arXiv:2609.11189v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.11189v1), to the exact [original matrix statement](README.md).

Set $v_j=A_{\cdot j}$. The hypothesis is precisely $\|v_j\|_2\le1$, and the theorem gives signs with $\|\sum_j\varepsilon_jv_j\|_\infty<3\sqrt{2\pi}$. This is the requested universal bound on $\|A\varepsilon\|_\infty$ for every allowed dimension.

The original theorem is by **Shengtao Guo, Ethan X. Fang and Junwei Lu**; Stepaniants authors the explanatory application note.
