# SP-12: full chromatic nullity target

The shared [final theorem](../../../../lean-statements/NLA/Proofs/SP12/Final.lean)
inhabits the unchanged `NLA.Statements.SP12.Target`. It constructs, for every
positive-order finite simple graph, a real symmetric matrix with exactly the
graph's off-diagonal nonzero pattern, a nonnegative quadratic form, the Strong
Arnold property and nullity at least the actual chromatic number minus one.
The final theorem has no extra premise. Local kernel checks report only
`propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

The self-contained [canonical package](../../../../eigenvalues-and-inverse-problems/SP-12/lean/README.md)
is prepared for the real isolated Linux Comparator. Until that gate and its
independent retained-artifact audit pass, the canonical page retains Solved.
IDs, canonical paths, original statements, original documents and all existing
mathematical attributions are preserved.

## Construction and independent source evidence

The actual shared polynomial LSS factor from SP-11 is retained. Its nonzero
specialized output forces independence of its actual earlier-nonneighbor
input rows. A greatest-nonzero-column argument then proves the exact SAP
constraints. This supplies actual PSD/SAP Strong Delta factors, including an
empty auxiliary graph. [Representation and discrete source review](REPRESENTATION_DISCRETE_ROOT_REVIEW_2026-10-18.md)
and [support review 1](SUPPORT_SOURCE_REVIEW_1.md) distinguish authorship from
independent checking. The [independent fresh support closure](SUPPORT_REVIEW_2_2026-10-18.md)
checks every explicit proof declaration with kernel trust and audits theorem
constant kinds; its [SP-11 prerequisite identity check](verification/SP11-prerequisite-identity.json)
compares all reused inputs to the actual Linux-accepted complete SP-11 proof.

The nonedge Gram map has its actual quadratic strict derivative. A functional
annihilating that derivative defines a symmetric matrix supported on nonedges;
the exact pattern and SAP force it to vanish, proving surjectivity. The inverse
function theorem supplies nearby factors with precisely the small cancelling
nonedge entries. A fixed test space and openness preserve SAP while one small
nonzero coordinate creates the new vertex's edges. The [analytic source review](ANALYTIC_ROOT_REVIEW_2026-10-18.md)
checks these three modules independently; the [raw direct compiler evidence](analytic-root-review-evidence-2026-10-18/result.json)
is distinct from a full fresh-closure or Linux check.

The discrete induction either applies Strong Delta directly or deletes a
low-degree vertex while preserving the actual chromatic number, recursively
constructs the smaller factor, and restores the vertex. Ambient dimension and
order both increase by one. Rank is at most the actual factor's column count.
Final explicitly supplies both proved constructions to that induction.
The [independent extension/final/package review](EXTENSION_FINAL_REVIEW_2_2026-10-18.md)
approves that exact assembly and all 51 source copies, with raw local checks of
the full shared Final and canonical Solution. It explicitly excludes the
reviewer's own four implemented modules from independent approval. The normal
shared CI aggregate imports SP12.Final, while the dedicated Linux package check
compares exactly the canonical export.

There are twelve new proof modules and thirty-six reused SP-11 proof modules,
plus three statement/infrastructure modules. The source-copy closure and
canonical wrapper are locked separately from the earlier boundary scaffold.
Compiled objects and dependency materializations are excluded from Git.
The [reproducible preservation checker](check-preservation.py) checks all 217
canonical pages and their immediate original artifacts against the recorded
SP-11 branch base. Historical reports are kept under separate filenames.

## Credit and limits of evidence

H. Tracy Hall is credited for the mathematical construction and Strong Delta
theorem; George Stepaniants authored the retained explanatory application note.
Formalization, packaging and reviews are AI-agent work, not external human
peer review. The formal proof supplies the required constructions explicitly
rather than assuming the paper's theorem. Its Gram-factor local-surjectivity
route avoids formalizing a rank-stratum manifold, and its finite graph induction
avoids a minimal-subgraph choice; both prove the unchanged original target.
Local kernel compilation and the SP-11 receipt do not certify the committed
SP-12 package's Linux Comparator acceptance. That receipt remains a separate gate.
