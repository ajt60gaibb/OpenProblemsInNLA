Every pairing is complex bilinear with no conjugation. The nonisotropic condition is `ψᵀψ≠0`, so both factor quadratic forms are nonzero.

At such a point, the slice `aᵀu=bᵀv=0` is an honest complement to the two projective radial directions. `waveFirst` and `waveSecond` are the first and mixed second variations of `a⊗b`. The quotient denominator has zero first variation on this slice, so `Critical` correctly tests vanishing of the restricted differential by requiring `ψᵀHδψ=0` for every tangent direction. At a critical point, the mixed Hessian of `N/D` is a nonzero scalar multiple of `D(δψᵀHδ′ψ+ψᵀHδ²ψ)−N(δψᵀδ′ψ+ψᵀδ²ψ)`, exactly `HessianNumerator`. Its radical condition against all tangent directions is the singular restricted Hessian, and the nonzero slice vector excludes the trivial radical element.

This defines the reduced projective hypersurface degree, rather than assigning a number to an opaque degree oracle or counting a repeated eliminant. The target has both formulas for every n≥2, with no finite test range.
