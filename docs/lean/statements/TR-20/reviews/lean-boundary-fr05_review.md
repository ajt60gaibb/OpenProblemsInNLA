`Sym2 (Fin m × Fin n)` supplies precisely one parameter per unordered pair of ordinary Segre entry coordinates, so `symmetricMatrix` is a full complex symmetric `mn×mn` matrix with no missing or extra constraints. `wave` is exactly `vec(abᵀ)` and the two bilinear pairings use plain complex multiplication, with no conjugation. `denominator` is `ψᵀψ`; its nonzero test excludes both factor-isotropic boundaries.

On that nonisotropic locus, the slices `aᵀu=0` and `bᵀv=0` remove precisely the two projective radial directions and represent the entire `m+n−2` dimensional Segre tangent space. `Critical` makes the quotient differential vanish on every such direction. `waveFirst` and `waveSecond` include both mixed product terms. `HessianNumerator` is the source's restricted bilinear quotient Hessian up to the nonzero scalar `2/(ψᵀψ)^2` at a critical point; the nonzero tangent radical criterion is therefore equivalent to Hessian singularity. The test is not an ambient or Hermitian Hessian.

`HasNonisotropicDegenerateCritical` requires nonzero `H` and a point with nonzero denominator. `VanishesOnIncidence` quantifies all polynomials vanishing on the exact incidence image; `InDiscriminantClosure` is their common zero set, the Zariski closure in the full symmetric parameter affine cone. `HasReducedHypersurfaceDegree` requires that **entire** vanishing ideal to be principal, generated by a nonzero nonunit homogeneous polynomial of the stated total degree. Because a vanishing ideal is radical, this enforces the reduced projective hypersurface degree rather than eliminant multiplicity, and excludes hidden lower-dimensional components. It does not substitute a generic matrix, arbitrary `(2,2)` section space, or an opaque degree oracle.

`Target` keeps both formulas for every `n≥2`: `24*choose(n+1,3)` for `m=2` and `24*n²*choose(n,2)` for `m=3`, including `n=2`.
