## Checks

The target keeps every `n ≥ 1` and every ordered nonnegative real spectrum,
including zero and repeated eigenvalues. It demands an explicit spectral
answer attained on the unitary orbit and bounding every unitary value; merely
rephrasing the maximum is expressly excluded. The orbit uses complex unitary
conjugate transpose, while the permanent is the full permutation product.

The specialized critical-value ideal has precisely the four source families:
trace powers for `1 ≤ k ≤ n`, the square-free minimal spectral polynomial
evaluated at the variable matrix, the matrix commutator with the permanental
adjoint, and `z−per(A)`. The adjoint's deleted-minor indices are transposed
correctly: row `j`, column `i` for entry `(i,j)`. The field is generated by
the actual input spectrum before elimination. The monic square-free generator
has positive degree; these properties must be established, not assumed as
premises to the main target.

The moment is the explicit finite Schur/character sum with partitions of
`np` of length at most `n`, the `kh` character product, positive Schur
denominators, and exact rational weights. The selector retains the finite
root list in `[0,L^n]`, zero and single-root branches, the adjacent gap,
least-integer `b`, ceiling `h`, order `p=2n²bh`, and strict comparison
`T>r_j^p`. Real critical roots that are not Hermitian-feasible remain in the
list and are handled by the moment test. No optimization, Haar integral, or
uncomputed limit is substituted for the finite answer.

The `n=1`, scalar-spectrum, zero-spectrum and source's `(1,3)` order-two
checks agree with the specification. The result asserts exact ordered-real
arithmetic on arbitrary real input; it makes no unsupported efficiency or
finite-precision claim. I found no substantive mismatch. Lean implementation
will still require concrete combinatorial/algebraic definitions, elaboration,
and two independent frozen-boundary reviews.
