## Original target and definitions

It does not limit width by seven, rank, order, or 444.

`SymMatrix` is the actual subtype `{A // A.IsSymm}`. I inspected the pinned Mathlib meanings of `Matrix.IsSymm` and the matrix topology: symmetry means transpose equality; the matrix topology is the product topology, inherited by the subtype. For finite real matrices this is the usual Euclidean topology on the symmetric-matrix space. The boundary is therefore not the spurious boundary of symmetric matrices inside all matrices. `CompletelyPositive` is the usual finite nonnegative real Gram-factor definition. Requiring positive width does not omit the zero matrix: a one-column zero factor suffices.
