\textbf{Rating rationale:} Historical rating of the original target.
Additivity across arbitrary polytopes posed a general structural barrier
for extension complexity, linking optimal factorizations with
compositional linear optimization models.

\textbf{Area:} nonnegative rank and polyhedral optimization

\subsection{Negative resolution --- 27 November
2026}\label{negative-resolution-27-november-2026}

Minhao Liu's \href{https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33631v1\#S1}{Theorem 1},
submitted 25 November 2026, constructs a full-dimensional rational
polytope \(P\subset\mathbb R^{24}\) with 50 facets and 150 vertices. The
extension complexities are 40 for \(P\) and 79 for \(P\times P\). If
\(S\) is the full facet--vertex slack matrix of \(P\) and \(C\) is the
matrix in the original statement with \(Q=P\), Yannakakis' factorization
theorem gives

\[\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits_+(C)=79
<80=2\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits_+(S).\]

\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2611.33631v1\#S5.SS3}{Section 5.3} supplies
the polytope inequalities. Proposition 9 gives the two real nonnegative
ranks for the selected rows, and Theorem 3 transfers them to the full
slack matrices by adding 26 for one factor and 52 for the square.

The
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/nonnegative-and-positive-factorizations/NR-02/solution.md}{independent
Codex proof note} verifies the strict gap with a limiting argument for
Liu's matrices and a direct proof that the retained coordinate rows are
facets. The review is informal and AI-assisted. Lluis Eriksson's
\href{https://airr.science/papers/ARR-2026-0WKHQBJJDA8C4VPT/versions/v1/}{companion
verification} checks selected finite steps using exact arithmetic and
Lean.

\subsection{Context and notation}\label{context-and-notation}

All factorizations are over the real numbers. For
\(X\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{m\times n}\), define

\[\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits_+(X)=\min\{r\ge0:X=WH,\quad
W\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{m\times r},\ H\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{r\times n}\}.\]

\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

Let \(P\subset\mathbb R^d\) and \(Q\subset\mathbb R^e\) be
full-dimensional polytopes with \(d,e\ge1\). Let
\(S\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{m\times n}\) and
\(T\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{p\times q}\) be their slack matrices: rows
correspond to all facets in irredundant inequality descriptions, columns
to all vertices, and each entry is the right-hand side minus the
left-hand side of that facet inequality at that vertex. Construct
\(C\in\mathbb R_{\ge0}^{(m+p)\times nq}\) by giving it one column for
each pair \((i,j)\) of vertices:

\[C[:,(i,j)]=\begin{pmatrix}S[:,i]\\T[:,j]\end{pmatrix}.\]

\subsubsection{Question}\label{question}

Is

\[\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits_+(C)=\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits_+(S)+\mathop{\mathrm{rank}}\nolimits_+(T)\]

always true? The hypotheses that \(S,T\) are polytope slack matrices are
part of the question. Through the slack-factorization theorem, this also
asks whether the minimum number of inequalities in an extended
formulation is additive under Cartesian products.

\subsection{Status check --- 2026-09-10}\label{status-check-2026-09-10}

Rechecked
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.01959}{Tiwary--Weltge--Zenklusen} and
\href{https://www.cargese.org/2022/open-problems.pdf}{Weltge's 2022
workshop question}, then searched for later product-additivity proofs
and counterexamples. Equality is proved whenever one factor is a
pyramid, a substantive portion of the displayed target. The unrestricted
equality remains open in the latest explicit source located; no
subsequent full resolution was found. The 2022 date limits the strength
of the current-status evidence.

\subsection{Reviewed continuation --- 13 September
2026}\label{reviewed-continuation-13-september-2026}

Sidney Holden (Center for Computational Biology, Flatiron Institute,
Simons Foundation) supplies a computer-assisted proof that
\(\mathop{\mathrm{xc}}\nolimits(P\times Q)=12\) whenever both factors
are polygons of extension complexity six. The
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/references/holden-continuations-2026-09-13/NR-02/report.pdf}{fourth-round
report}, main theorem and geometry-to-Boolean reduction, reduces this
statement to ten exact LRAT refutations. The broader inherited
all-polytopes corollary in the package was not independently re-audited
in this submission.

This was the basis for the September \textbf{Partially resolved}
classification. The
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/references/holden-continuations-2026-09-13/verification/NR-02/review.md}{independent
informal Codex AI-agent review} records its accepted scope and checks.
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/references/holden-continuations-2026-09-13/README.md}{Submission,
original package, reproduction and verified affiliation}. This is not
external human peer review or formal verification; no Lean verification
was performed.
