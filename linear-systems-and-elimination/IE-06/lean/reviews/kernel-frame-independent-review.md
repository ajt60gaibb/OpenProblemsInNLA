Rank-nullity gives the exact kernel dimension s from surjectivity of the
actual m by (m+s) Euclidean map. A genuine orthonormal basis of that kernel
is reindexed by Fin s and embedded in the ambient coordinates. The proof
checks both Q^TQ=I and range(Q)=ker(M) by the basis representation map.
No measurable family of frames is claimed, and the zero-dimensional
kernel case is included. Approved exact statement and proof; independent
compilation is recorded separately.
