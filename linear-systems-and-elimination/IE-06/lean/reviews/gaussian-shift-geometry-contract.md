# Gaussian shift geometry: reviewed first unconditional step

The coordinator independently approved the following exact contracts before implementation on 2026-10-06. For real normed vector space E, sets K,C, and displacement v, define
$$
A_v=\{x:x\in K,\ x-v\in C\}.
$$
If K and C are centrally symmetric, reflection carries A_v to A_(-v). If K and C are convex, x in A_v and y in A_(-v) imply (x+y)/2 belongs to K intersection C. Consequently the set of all such midpoints is contained in K intersection C. If K,C are Borel measurable in finite Euclidean space, A_v is measurable and volume(A_v)=volume(A_(-v)) by measure-preservation of negation.

These are unconditional geometric and measure-theoretic facts, valid for empty sets and zero-dimensional Euclidean space. They are the geometric prerequisite to the midpoint Brunn–Minkowski plus Gaussian layer-cake route. They do not establish the required volume comparison or Gaussian shift inequality themselves.
