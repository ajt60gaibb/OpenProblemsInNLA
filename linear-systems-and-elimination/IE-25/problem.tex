\textbf{Rating rationale:} Controlling the perturbed factorization
uniformly for singular Neumann systems in two and three dimensions is
challenging. Community impact comes from a concrete preconditioner for
widely used PDE linear solves.

\subsection{Statement}\label{statement}

Fix \(d\in\{2,3\}\) and a bounded connected smooth domain
\(\Omega\subset\mathbb R^d\). On \(h\mathbb Z^d\), retain points whose
cubic control cells \(C_p=p+[-h/2,h/2]^d\) intersect \(\Omega\), ordered
lexicographically as \(1,\ldots,N_h\). For face neighbors set

\[w_{pq}=\frac{\mathop{\mathrm{vol}}\nolimits_{d-1}(C_p\cap C_q\cap\Omega)}{h^{d-1}},\]

and set other weights to zero. Let \(a_p=\sum_qw_{pq}\), \(A_{pp}=a_p\),
\(A_{pq}=-w_{pq}\) for \(p\ne q\), and let \(L\) be the strictly lower
triangular part of \(A\). This is the Purvis--Burkhalter Neumann
discretization, with length fractions in two dimensions and face-area
fractions in three dimensions.

For \(\varepsilon>0\), define
\(E=\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}\nolimits(e_1,\ldots,e_{N_h})\) recursively by

\[e_1=a_1,\qquad
e_p=(1+\varepsilon)a_p-
\sum_{\substack{q< p\\w_{pq}>0}}
\frac{w_{pq}}{e_q}\sum_{s>q}w_{qs}\quad(p>1),\]

and \(M=(L+E)E^{-1}(E+L^T)\). This expresses the source's PMILU
recurrence by neighbor sums, including its explicitly unperturbed first
pivot. Its two-dimensional display is Definition 5; §4 states the
conjecture in both dimensions.

When the pivots are positive, let \(\kappa_+(M^{-1}A)\) be the ratio of
the largest to smallest positive eigenvalue of \(M^{-1/2}AM^{-1/2}\);
the zero eigenvalue from the Neumann nullspace is excluded.

\textbf{Conjecture.} There exist \(c_\Omega,K_\Omega,h_\Omega>0\),
independent of \(h\), such that choosing \(\varepsilon=c_\Omega h^2\)
gives positive pivots and

\[\kappa_+(M^{-1}A)\le K_\Omega h^{-1}
\qquad(0< h< h_\Omega).\]

The existence quantifier spells out the source's choice of ``some
moderate constant''; it does not impose an invented interval for that
constant. The authors suggest the practical choice \(c_\Omega=1\).

\subsection{Numerical significance}\label{numerical-significance}

The question concerns a concrete diagonal perturbation of incomplete
factorization for singular Neumann systems. It is separate from relaxed
ILU: the diagonal recurrence differs, and the proposed PMILU bound
includes three dimensions.

\subsection{Positive pivots and a uniform lower spectral
bound}\label{positive-pivots-and-a-uniform-lower-spectral-bound}

\textbf{Partial result (AI-derived).} In both dimensions of the
statement, the PMILU pivots are positive for all \(\varepsilon>0\) and
sufficiently small \(h\). For \(\varepsilon=ch^2\) with any fixed
\(c>0\), the smallest positive generalized eigenvalue stays bounded away
from zero uniformly in \(h\). The upper spectral bound needed for the
conjectured condition number remains open.

The positive-weight face graph is connected and has more than one vertex
for small \(h\). The path proof and the
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/linear-systems-and-elimination/IE-24/README.md\#weighted-poincaré-estimate-for-the-cut-cell-graph}{weighted
Poincaré estimate} apply to the stated square or cubic control cells.
Put \(s_p=\sum_{q>p}w_{pq}\) and \(k_p=e_p-s_p\). The unperturbed first
pivot is \(e_1=s_1=a_1>0\), so \(k_1=0\). For \(p>1\) the PMILU
recurrence gives

\[k_p=\varepsilon a_p+\sum_{q< p,\,w_{pq}>0}\frac{w_{pq}}{e_q}k_q.\]

Since \(a_p>0\) at every vertex of this connected graph, induction gives
\(k_p>0\) for all \(p>1\) and positive pivots throughout.

Expanding the factors gives
\(M=A+\mathop{\mathrm{diag}}\nolimits(M\mathbf1)-L_F\), where \(L_F\) is
the graph Laplacian with nonnegative fill weights
\(F_{ps}=\sum_{q<\min(p,s)}w_{pq}w_{sq}/e_q\) for \(p\ne s\). The row
sums are \((M\mathbf1)_1=0\) and \((M\mathbf1)_p=\varepsilon a_p\) for
\(p>1\). The exception is exactly the unperturbed first pivot in the
displayed recurrence. The row sums follow by subtracting \(a_p\) from
\(e_p\) and adding \(\sum_{q< p,\,w_{pq}>0}w_{pq}s_q/e_q\).

For \(x\) in the \(M\)-orthogonal complement of the constants,
\(x^TMx\le (x-b\mathbf1)^TM(x-b\mathbf1)\) for every constant \(b\).
Choose \(b\) minimizing \(\sum_p a_p|x_p-b|^2\). The fill Laplacian is
positive semidefinite and \(A\mathbf1=0\), so the factor identity and
the weighted Poincaré estimate give

\[x^TMx\le x^TAx+\varepsilon \sum_p a_p|x_p-b|^2
      \le (1+cC_\Omega)x^TAx.\]

The generalized Rayleigh principle yields
\(\lambda_{\min,+}(M^{-1}A)\ge(1+cC_\Omega)^{-1}\).
