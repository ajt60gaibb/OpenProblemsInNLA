# Existing formalization study

The statement/proof organization was compared with the audited MI-19, MF-05,
MF-07, RA-02, RA-03, RA-08, RA-09, IS-03, NM-04, PF-02, MI-21 and MI-22
formalizations in the repository. We reuse their statement-first convention,
separate Challenge/Solution files, explicit `#print axioms` reports, pinned
toolchain metadata and `formalization.yaml` fields. No mathematical theorem or
source file is imported from those developments. Their probability examples do
not provide a varying-dimensional Haar-sphere product law suitable for IE-21.
