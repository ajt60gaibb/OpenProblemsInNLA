# Open Problems in Numerical Linear Algebra

A developing collection of **1,000 precise open problems** drawn from NLA books
and research publications, with references and dated checks for subsequent
solutions. The target is 1,000; the collection is not yet complete.

Every problem will include a mathematical statement, source references, and:

- **Difficulty:** hard, challenging, or extreme.
- **Importance:** interesting to specialist, interesting to the community, or
  broadly interesting.

The ratings are editorial judgments. Historical conjectures that have been
resolved and candidates whose status remains uncertain do not count as admitted
open problems. No solutions are being attempted.

The catalog is being populated under `proof/catalog/`. See [the exact
objective](PROBLEM.md) and [current progress](PROGRESS.md).

The existing `lean/` workspace is inactive. `AGENTS.md` records the repository's
standing research rules; review-channel files are retained for future use.
