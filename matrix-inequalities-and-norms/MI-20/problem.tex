Expanding the structural non-attainment manuscript below (and fixing the
singular-vector direction slip in Theorem 2, Step 1), Version 4 proves a
fixed-dimension unitary-free PSD/POVM reformulation via Same-Dimension
Lemma 2' (Theorem 5), formalizes Holden's complex-interpolation
log-convexity at fixed finite dimension \(n\) (Theorem 6), proves a
concentration-compactness dichotomy ruling out mass splitting in
\((\mathcal{S}_p(\ell^2))^m\) (Theorem 7), establishes the exact
two-term \(p \to 1^+\) quadratic law in dimension \(2\) (Proposition 8),
and shows unconditionally that any attaining tuple in
\((\mathcal{S}_p(\mathcal{H}))^m\) satisfies
\(\ker(S) \cap \overline{\mathop{\mathrm{ran}}\nolimits(H)} = \{0\}\),
onto-isometric polar factors
\(U_k U_k^* = I_{\overline{\mathop{\mathrm{ran}}\nolimits(H)}}\), and
\(0 \in \sigma(H^{-1/2}|A_k|H^{-1/2})\) (Theorem 8).

On Holden's positive power system
\((D + H_j)^{2\kappa-1} = \Lambda D^{1/2} G_j^{\kappa-1} D^{1/2}\)
(\(\kappa = \frac{p}{2(p-1)}\),
\(\Phi_j = G_j^\kappa - D G_j^{\kappa-1} D\)), Theorem 9 proves the
commutator identity \([D, \Phi_j] = 0\) for each summand individually, a
Power-Variance Lemma, and a Top-Eigenspace Spectral Dichotomy on
\(E_{\max} = \ker(D - d_{\max}I)\). Four progressively sharper
pure-algebraic universal upper bounds hold for all \(1 < p < 2\) and
\(m \ge 2\) (all Lean 4 verified): Theorem 10 proves
\(\sigma_+(\Lambda) \le (m-1)\frac{(\kappa-1)^{\kappa-1}}{\kappa^\kappa}\)
and the first dimension-free non-asymptotic quadratic bound
\(\ln C_{p,m}(\infty) \le \frac{m^2-1}{2p^2}(p-1)^2\) with
Lambert-\(W\) constant \(K_m\) (\(K_2 \approx 0.317236\)); Theorem 10A
strengthens this in one line to
\((1+h_+(\Lambda))^{2\kappa-1}(h_+(\Lambda)-1) \le (m-1)\frac{(2\kappa-1)^{2\kappa-1}}{\kappa^{2\kappa}}\),
which is exact in the Frobenius limit \(p \to 2^-\) for every
\(m \ge 2\) and beats Holden at \(p = 3/2, 5/4, 5/3, 7/4\) with zero
SDP; Theorem 10B establishes a coupled two-function bound proving the
\(C^\infty\)-flat super-exponential collapse law
\(0 < C_{p,2}(\infty) - C_{p,2}(2) = O\bigl((1+\sqrt{2})^{-\frac{2(p-1)}{2-p}}\bigr)\)
as \(p \to 2^-\) (pinning \(C_{1.95,2}(\infty)\) to 14 digits,
\(C_{1.90,2}(\infty)\) to 10 digits, \(C_{1.85,2}(\infty)\) to 7 digits,
and giving \(C_{7/4,2}(\infty) \le 1.0681498\) and
\(C_{5/3,2}(\infty) \le 1.0574723\) with zero SDP); and Theorem 10C
proves a 2-line pure-algebraic Bessel bound at \(p = 4/3\) for all
\(m \ge 2\) (\(C_{4/3,2}(\infty) \le 1.018930\),
\(C_{4/3,3}(\infty) \le 1.032735\)).

Theorem 11 constructs self-similar infinite-tree extremizers on
\(\ell^2(\mathcal{T}_s)\) whose depth-2 (\(42\times 42\)) truncations
yield zero-float certified lower bounds
\(C_{4/3,2}(\infty) > 1.0183715755\) and
\(C_{3/2,2}(\infty) > 1.03643716806\). Theorem 12 proves an
unconditional Two-State Pinch 2-letter reduction together with strict
convexity
\(\sigma_+'(\Lambda) = \bigl(\frac{h_+(\Lambda)}{1+h_+(\Lambda)}\bigr)^{2\kappa-2}\);
exact rational \(LDL^\top\) four-corner rectangle and convex-trapezoid
SOS certificates yield
\(C_{4/3,2}(\infty) \le (21511/20000)^{1/4} \approx 1.0183748167\)
(\(52.1\times\) tighter gap than Holden's \((861/800)^{1/4}\)) and
\(C_{3/2,2}(\infty) \le (890677/800000)^{1/3} \approx 1.0364382131\)
(\(7{,}795\times\) tighter gap than Holden's interpolated bound, width
\(1.05\times 10^{-6}\)), strictly tightening the best known certified
upper bound across every \(1 < p < 2\) at \(m = 2\). Theorem 13 derives
the exact \(p \to 1^+\) Baker--Campbell--Hausdorff operator limit
\(\operatorname{Dexp}_{-M_j}[U_j^2] = 4a_L e^{-M_j} - 2\Phi_{\lim,j}\)
(\(K_2^{\mathrm{tree}}(\infty) = 0.2259284\dots\)) and the sharp
\(\mathcal{S}_p\)-tree summability criterion
\((m-1)\sum_{i=2}^s d_i^{2\kappa} < 1\). Theorems 1, 3 (\(1 < p < 2\)
and \(p = 3/2\)), 4 (\(p = 3/2\)), 5, 6 (from \(p_0 = 1\)), 9, 10, 10A,
10B, and 10C (40 top-level theorems across 11 modules, 13,770 lines) are

\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

For a complex square matrix \(X\), write \(|X|=(X^*X)^{1/2}\) and
\(\|X\|_p=(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits|X|^p)^{1/p}\). For every
integer \(m\ge2\) and real \(1< p<2\), determine exactly the
dimension-independent constant

\[C_p(m)=\sup_{n\ge1}\ \sup_{A_1,\ldots,A_m\in\mathbb C^{n\times n},\,\sum_j|A_j|\ne0}
\frac{\|\sum_{j=1}^m A_j\|_p}{\|\sum_{j=1}^m|A_j|\|_p}.\]

Thus the requested answer is the optimal constant, as a function of both
\(p\) and \(m\), in the corresponding Schatten norm inequality. An upper
bound alone does not settle the problem. All matrix orders are included
in the same supremum.

These constants quantify the loss when replacing a matrix sum by the sum
of its positive semidefinite moduli, an operation common in norm
estimates for matrix computations. This is distinct from constants
comparing the arithmetic and symmetric moduli of one matrix (catalog
MI-09).
