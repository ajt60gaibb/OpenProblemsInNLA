`v₁ = R/(b+aR)` and its second at `v₂ = a+bR`.

The substitutions v = γ/(b+aγ) and v = a+bγ then each map [1,R] exactly to the relevant truncated interval. For D(σ||M), exchange a,b and ρ,σ. The resulting coefficients combine to the exact C12 kernels. C13's first primitive is a(log γ−log(b+aγ)); its value at 1 is zero because a+b = 1. On [1,R], 1 ≤ R/(b+aR) ≤ 1/a, yielding the first nonnegative C14 bound, and similarly for the other. C09 integrated against these positive kernels yields C15 using one uniform cutoff and finite differences. No interchange of entropy differentiation with a positive-part integral is needed.
