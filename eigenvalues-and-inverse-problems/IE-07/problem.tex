\subsection{Resolution --- 2026-11-24}\label{resolution-2026-11-24}

\textbf{Solved affirmatively by Matthew J. Colbrook}, Department of
Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics, University of Cambridge.
\textbf{Theorem 1.1 (Deterministic spectral regularisation)} of
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.28614}{\emph{Deterministic
regularisation of the nonsymmetric eigenproblem}}, submitted 21 November
2026, resolves the full original exact-arithmetic target. The proof is
in Sections 2--7, with the algorithm, termination, uniform bounds and
arithmetic cost assembled in Section 7.

For every \(n\geq2\), complex contraction \(A\) and \(0<\delta<1/2\),
the theorem constructs deterministically \(E\) with \(\|E\|_2<\delta\)
such that \(A+E\) has simple spectrum and

\[\frac{\kappa_V(A+E)}{\mathop{\mathrm{gap}}\nolimits(A+E)}
\leq C(n/\delta)^{20/3},\]

using \(O(n^3\log^d(n/\delta))\) real-field operations and comparisons,
for universal \(C,d\). It also returns an exact eigensystem and inverse
eigenbasis. Thus it supplies the required perturbation, including for
defective inputs, with \(c=20/3\) and a strictly stronger perturbation
bound. The definitions of the spectral norm, eigenbasis condition number
and minimum gap agree with the retained statement below.

The computational model is specified immediately after Theorem 1.1 and
in Section 6: arithmetic and comparisons have unit cost, including
operations on integers of arbitrary length. The result concerns the
exact-arithmetic target; it does not assert a floating-point precision
or bit-complexity bound. The status records the cited arXiv result,
without asserting a new independent proof audit or Lean verification by
this catalog. Section 1.3 discloses AI assistance.

The permanent ID, canonical path, original statement, source references
and earlier audit notes are retained. The difficulty and importance
ratings are historical; this entry is excluded from the open count.

\subsection{Problem statement}\label{problem-statement}

For a matrix \(M\) with simple spectrum, define

\[\mathop{\mathrm{gap}}\nolimits(M)=\min_{i\ne j}|\lambda_i(M)-\lambda_j(M)|,
\qquad
\kappa_V(M)=\inf_{M=VDV^{-1},\ D\text{ diagonal}}\|V\|_2\|V^{-1}\|_2.\]

For every \(n\geq2\), \(A\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}\) with
\(\|A\|_2\leq1\), and \(0<\delta<1/2\), construct deterministically a
matrix \(E\) such that

\[\|E\|_2\leq\delta,\qquad A+E\text{ has simple spectrum},\qquad
\frac{\kappa_V(A+E)}{\mathop{\mathrm{gap}}\nolimits(A+E)}\leq C(n/\delta)^c,\]

using \(O(n^3\log^d(n/\delta))\) exact arithmetic operations, for
universal constants \(C,c,d\). The task is to find the perturbation, not
merely prove its existence.
