# Tensor, positive-factorization and complexity audit — 2026-09-10

All 38 assigned canonical statements were read in full against the repository rating rubric. Each ID received targeted searches for later solutions and inspection of the primary status sources linked below. “Open” means no full resolution was located within that bounded evidence; it is not a proof of absence. “Partially resolved” identifies substantive proved portions of the displayed target, even when known before this audit. Weaker bounds, neighboring models and numerical evidence alone do not trigger that label.

**Results:** 20 Open, 18 Partially resolved. No full solution, full-solution claim, withdrawal or material statement-fidelity obstacle was found for these 38 entries. Equations and target quantifiers were preserved.

**Rating changes:** NM-01 and NM-03 move from extreme / broadly interesting to challenging / interesting to the community; TR-13, TR-14 and TR-16 move from community to specialist importance; AC-07 and AC-08 move from challenging to extreme. These are editorial recalibrations: focused constrained-factorization questions differ from foundational complexity barriers, while the two addition-chain conjectures have resisted decades of work. Every entry now gives a rationale for both ratings.

**Material distinctions:** TR-09’s VALG result uses additional assumptions and a changed algorithmic pipeline, so its claimed partial progress is not treated as a verified solution under all displayed conventions. NM-04’s general algebraic degree bound is solved but its coefficient identity remains open beyond proved sizes. NR-03 concerns a fixed completion, whereas the July 2026 unique-disjointness paper allows unspecified entries. AC-02/03, PF-03 and AC-10 preserve their exact field and rank notions.

**New useful sources:** Alman–Li 2026 explicitly reaffirms AC-02 as well as AC-03. Wallner’s April 2026 journal paper gives a stronger current-status source for AC-07 than a standalone manuscript. The July 2026 sumset-expansion paper provides a concrete excluded semi-explicit construction for AC-06. Noferini’s December 2025 seminar reaffirms NM-03, while Gouveia–Wiebe 2026 studies a distinct integer-factor task.
