# Mathematical catalog workspace

[Browse the open problems](../CATALOG.md), or consult the [source coverage,
exclusions, and uncounted leads](SOURCES.md).

Canonical problem statements and their LaTeX/PDF versions live in category
folders at the repository root. `catalog/` retains the former chapter anchors,
links to the relocated problems, shared source notation, and uncounted notes.

The current task is curation and maintenance of the 159 admitted statements, preserving
their references, ratings, and checks for existing solutions. No proof development or Lean
formalization is requested. The full objective is recorded in
[PROBLEM.md](../PROBLEM.md).

The [additional-problems screen](ADDITIONAL-PROBLEMS-SCREEN.md) maps every candidate
in the preserved user proposal to its admitted entry or exclusion reason.

The [further literature screen](LITERATURE-EXPANSION-2026-09.md) records the latest
12 additions, their primary source locators, and uncounted reserves.
