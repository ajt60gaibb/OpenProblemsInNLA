# Literature expansion to 200 open targets — 2026-09-10

This screen admits **42 new problems**. The starting catalog had 159 open
targets; the concurrent status audit settles former MI-13 and removes it from
the open count. Thus **159 − 1 + 42 = 200**. Retained resolved or claimed-solved
pages are additional archival entries, not part of the 200.

The search examined primary research papers, author preprints, surveys,
book chapters and a doctoral thesis. It revisited older problem lists against
current versions and 2025–September 2026 publications. It does not claim to have
read every NLA book or to establish the universal absence of a solution.
Each admitted page supplies the complete statement, editorial ratings and
rationale, exact references, known cases and the limits of its dated search.

Three parallel searches covered matrix inequalities/functions, randomized and
tensor methods, and structured/interval/iterative methods. An additional source
screen covered graph inverse eigenvalues, permanents and phase retrieval.
Independent cross-checks scrutinized the final statements and later results;
the concurrent full-catalog audit also reviews these admissions. No solutions
were attempted in this expansion. MI-13's short status argument belongs to the
separately requested audit and is documented on its retained page.

## Source fidelity and distinctness

- Generalized quadratic phase measurements (FR-11) and linear low-rank
  measurements (RA-17) ask for different recovery models. Real/complex variants
  of the former are grouped. The latter retains only the unresolved real exact
  minimum; the complex count is already known. Almost-everywhere recovery is not
  recovery of every input.
- TR-22 asks whether a fixed tensor format has finitely many asymptotic-rank
  values. TR-23 asks whether every asymptotic-rank sublevel variety is
  irreducible. The source poses these separately and explains a one-way
  implication from irreducibility to discreteness. They concern different
  objects and are not asserted to be equivalent; neither arbitrary parameter
  instances nor stronger numerical bounds on one invariant are counted twice.
- TR-24 asks for ideal generation in degrees five, six and nine, not the solved
  set-theoretic Salmon statement. No unsupported number of generators is given.
  TR-25 concerns the depth of coordinate rings, a different geometric property.
- TR-26 is the rational-normal-curve discriminant question, distinct from the
  Segre-variety degree question in TR-20. Its critical-correspondence closure
  retains isotropic boundary points. TR-31 uses affine cone dimensions
  34, 50, 64 and 74 for the four exceptions; a missing cone-dimension term in one
  later survey display is not propagated.
- The weighted mean parameters in MI-21 and MI-23 remain full single questions.
  MI-23 uses the authors' corrected eigenvalue conjecture; their unrestricted
  earlier singular-value conjecture is refuted. The proved eigenvalue-modulus
  analogue associated with MI-22 changes both sides, so it is not presented as
  a logical weakening of its singular-value statement.
- MI-28–29 follow the thesis's final Problems 1–2. These passages correct a
  differing base power and broaden a matrix class from earlier chapter wording.
  The already solved squared-base cases are not separate entries. MI-27 keeps
  the proposed sharp coefficient one; existence of a universal coefficient is
  solved. MI-26 retains real-valued concave functions without silently imposing
  global nonnegativity.
- IV-03 is a recognition criterion, while IV-05 computes a solution hull under
  a promise of inverse-M membership. IV-04 asks for the exact hull for arbitrary
  tridiagonal interval systems, including emptiness and unboundedness. These are
  not duplicates of the catalog's determinant-range question.
- IE-24–25 state different incomplete-factorization recurrences. Their Neumann
  nullspace is excluded from the spectral condition number; the parameter
  constants are independent of mesh width. The source's unperturbed first pivot
  is preserved. Results for SPD or Dirichlet systems do not settle these targets.
- MF-21 keeps all three expansion assertions for all higher-order zeros together.
  MF-22 reproduces one complete source-selected block-Toeplitz family, including
  all coefficient matrices and their order. Polynomial growth means fixed
  parameter and constants independent of matrix size. No generic theorem is
  inferred from determinant-root counts alone.
- Matrix and scalar-recurrence versions of Skolem's problem are one entry;
  bounded/unbounded continuous-time variants are likewise grouped. Conditional
  decidability is not unconditional decidability.

## Uncounted reserves and access limits

General recurrence Positivity, Ultimate Positivity, two-dimensional mortality,
and Chow-secant nondefectivity were sourced but not added in this batch. Their
absence is not a claim of resolution. No extra cases of already counted rank,
RIP, matrix-sensing, Toeplitz or matrix-mean questions were used to reach the count.

Further leads were withheld for specific gaps: strong interval singularity needs
reconciliation with matrix-completion results; an AVE inverse-nonnegative
converse needs the exact scope of a 2026 paper; a strongly polynomial regularity
radius needs an unambiguous arithmetic model; NIPALS and structured backward-error
directions need complete quantitative targets; the broad block-Toeplitz
root-count proposal needs additional structural scrutiny. The previous tensor
rank-increment reserve still lacks a sufficiently accessible exact source.

MI-22's 2026 reaffirmation was read from publicly indexed text of the primary
paper because its publisher PDF was inaccessible. MI-28–29 use the author's
uploaded thesis. IE-24–25 compare the available 2025 preprint and the 2026 journal
abstract; full journal text was inaccessible. Older-source entries state when no
recent explicit reaffirmation was found. These limits are retained in the
canonical pages, not replaced by claims of exhaustive verification.

## Presentation and contributions

Every new entry uses the repository's Markdown, standalone LaTeX and PDF
formats. Final rendering and page inspection are integrated with the concurrent
status audit and recorded in `PROGRESS.md`. The root catalog is framed as a
maintained collection of 200 open targets. Contributor instructions explain
primary-source requirements, duplicate and resolution checks, ratings, issues,
pull requests and regeneration of human-readable documents. Issue and PR
templates make these requirements visible when proposing a change.
