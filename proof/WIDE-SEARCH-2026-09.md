# Wider source screen — September 2026

The user requested 50 further precise NLA conjectures and six search subagents.
Six searches were launched, covering iterative methods, matrix functions and
inequalities, randomized NLA and frames, tensors, spectral/inverse problems,
and structured matrices. The starting catalog contained 75 entries. This note
records substantive source and status findings. **50 new entries are admitted,
bringing the catalog from 75 to 125.** The six searches ran in two waves because
three subagents could run concurrently. Root review checked every selected draft;
independent audits additionally checked the tensor and spectral/structured groups.

The scope includes source-stated conjectures and precisely posed exact-value
or sharp-order open questions. An optimization question is identified as such,
rather than attributed an answer that its author did not conjecture. Matrix
theory and frame-design entries explain their connection to NLA. None of the
questions is solved here.

## Resolutions, withdrawals, and withheld formulations

- **General Lyapunov symmetric maximizer:** the [August 2026 counterexample](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20875) refutes every order at least seven. Only its explicitly unresolved order six is retained.
- **Complementary McCarthy inequality:** [2608.20937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20937) reports the remaining negative-exponent resolution. Hanner's inequality has a resolution through Heinävaara's [tracial joint spectral measures](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.03227); neither old question is added.
- **Tang–Zhang's proposed Schatten constant formula:** [Zeng–Liu–Ratnavelu, August 2026](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.1067), Theorem 1, supplies a counterexample. It does not invalidate the separate arithmetic-symmetric-modulus questions.
- **Quadratic versus arithmetic symmetric modulus:** [2606.29188](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29188), Theorem 1.1, already proves the quadratic two-unitary inequality. A conflicting formula in Conjecture 3.13 of [2606.15624](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.15624) is not copied; MI-06 follows Zhang's original arithmetic-modulus statement.
- **Vinzant's finite-dimensional probability inequality:** [Li, 2606.17922](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17922) proves the first part, $p_d<1$. It does not give the limit asserted in FR-05.
- **Gaussian phase-retrieval deterioration:** [Shmalo, 2607.06249](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.06249) resolves the critical Gaussian base. A theorem for that distribution does not prove FR-04's bound for every deterministic full-spark frame.
- **SIC universal proof claim withdrawn:** the latest [2601.13475v5 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.13475) states that its proof is incorrect. Its unchanged abstract is not a resolution of FR-07.
- **Deterministic log-free $\ell_p$ embedding claim withdrawn:** the latest [2601.00361v3 record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.00361) says proofs are incomplete. The claim is not used to exclude Yasuda's earlier questions. Those additional embedding questions were not drafted in this batch.
- **Complex Hadamard matrices of order six:** the [August classification claim](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.18053), §VI, explicitly leaves the mutually unbiased bases problem unsettled. It therefore does not remove FR-06.
- **Anderson limiting directions:** Conjecture 6 in [2312.04776v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.04776v4) literally uses unnormalized iterates, while its surrounding explanation and plots suggest directions. A normalized convergence conjecture is withheld because choosing sphere or projective convergence would change the printed assertion.
- **Other withheld formulations:** Balan–Wang's fixed-additive-redundancy Conjecture 5.2 has a clear numbered formula but conflicting adjacent prose and no recent reaffirmation located. The seven-product polynomial claim is used without splitting off ambiguous six-product real/complex claims. Vague preconditioning extensions, unspecified random Vandermonde models, and an insufficiently checked general Toeplitz-factor statement remain outside the count.

- **Tensor eigenvalue algebraic/geometric multiplicity:** [2609.01268](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01268) reports a general resolution. The historical Qi and Hu–Ye formulations are not added.
- **Ordinary Comon and symmetric generic identifiability:** the unrestricted exact-rank Comon conjecture is refuted, while symmetric generic-identifiability classifications have later theorems. Neither removes the different nonsymmetric, border, or Hankel-restricted conjectures admitted here.
- **Salmon ideal conjecture:** Friedland–Gross's [2011 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.1776), §1, states revised degree-five/six/nine ideal generation, while its theorem proves only equality of zero sets. A [2026 primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.24879v1), §1.5.3, retains the ideal question. This precise reserve was not selected for this batch; it is not marked resolved.
- **Chollet through order six:** the [August 31 preprint](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202608.2196) claims low-order progress, not all orders. The [stable-polynomial counterexample](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.29595v1), §3.1, refutes a stronger statement and is not a PSD-matrix counterexample to Lieb's conjecture.
- **Other spectral candidates:** q-permanent monotonicity, its subset-stabilizer inequality, graph-complement minimum rank, the delta minimum-rank conjecture, and a chromatic bound for PSD/SAP nullity were drafted but not selected. Circulant Hadamard is withheld because standing full-proof claims require further resolution; it is not conflated with universal Hadamard existence.

- **Hillar's determinant conjecture:** Minghua Lin's proof, presented as Theorem 3.5 in [Hillar–Wibisono (2015)](https://redwood.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/hillar2015hadamard.pdf), excludes the determinant question. IE-19 is its distinct inverse-norm companion.
- **Sinkhorn/Kruithof algebraic degree:** [Fang, 2506.06338v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2506.06338v2) proves the degree-bound consequence. Rowland's later talk still marks the coefficient identity conjectural. Coefficient subidentities and separate square/rectangular instances are not counted again.
- **Toeplitz corrected proof:** the authors' appended erratum explicitly withdraws the proof of the Jordan-curve-to-finite-real-spectrum implication. A later block generalization leaves curve multiplicity conventions unclear, so the scalar corrected conjecture is retained alone.
- **Remaining structured reserves:** a Kac–Murdock–Szegő contour conjecture has an additional low-magnitude clause with unresolved boundary conventions; the coherent high-magnitude part was drafted but not selected. Operator Sinkhorn unitary-orbit limits need a complete statement of the underlying flags and canonical marginals. Neither is counted.

## Limits of the screen

The primary statement and relevant current-version information were inspected
for the proposed additions. Older GMRES, LSMR, and Seelmann questions retain
explicitly weaker historical-source status evidence. Targeted searches and
partial-result comparisons are recorded in each entry. A standing full-solution
claim warrants withholding a question; the screen does not certify that claim's
proof. A withdrawn claim warrants a different disposition. No bounded search
can establish the universal absence of a later solution.
