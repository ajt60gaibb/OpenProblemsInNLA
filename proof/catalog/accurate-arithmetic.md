## Historical source lead — not counted

Higham's *Accuracy and Stability of Numerical Algorithms*, §11.1, and
S. H. Cheng's 1998 dissertation ask about sharp element growth in Aasen's
tridiagonal factorization. The old $4^{n-2}$ bound is **not** an unresolved
sharpness conjecture: Y. Feng and L. Lu,
[*On the growth factor upper bound for Aasen's algorithm*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.08994),
*Applied Mathematics Letters* 88 (2019), 118–124, replaces it by $2^{n-1}$
and shows that the new bound is itself not sharp for $n\ge6$.
A remaining exact-growth problem needs a fresh statement with precise pivot
and breakdown conventions and a fuller check of later work. No additional
problem is admitted from this lead yet.
