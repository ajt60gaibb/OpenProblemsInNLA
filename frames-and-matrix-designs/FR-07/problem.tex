\subsection{Resolution --- 2026-11-27}\label{resolution-2026-11-27}

\textbf{Solved affirmatively with Lean verification.} Section 9.2 of
\href{https://hexagonmath.org/2610.00186v1}{\emph{Stark units for real
quadratic fields and reciprocity laws}} by Danylo Radchenko and Campbell
Wheeler proves the existence assertion using the ghost-SIC construction
of Appleby, Flammia and Kopp. The preprint discloses AI assistance.
Yaoling Yang's
\href{https://palomar-registry.org/entry?id=PALOMAR-2026-11-26-000040&version=1}{all-dimension
Lean proof} was independently checked by Palomar on 26 November 2026.
Māris Ozols also produced an
\href{https://github.com/marozols/SIC-existence}{earlier independent
Lean development}. The
\href{https://github.com/ajt60gaibb/OpenProblemsInNLA/blob/main/frames-and-matrix-designs/FR-07\#lean-proof-and-verification-evidence}{verification
evidence} includes the argument that the formalized overlap condition
forces the stated tight-frame identity.

For every integer \(d\geq2\), do there exist \(d^2\) unit vectors
\(\phi_1,\ldots,\phi_{d^2}\in\mathbb C^d\) satisfying

\[|\phi_i^*\phi_j|^2=\frac1{d+1}\quad(i\ne j),
\qquad
\sum_{i=1}^{d^2}\phi_i\phi_i^*=dI_d?\]

This is existence of an equiangular tight frame with \(d^2\) vectors in
\(\mathbb C^d\), equivalently a symmetric informationally complete
positive operator-valued measure after dividing the rank-one projectors
by \(d\). The statement concerns existence in every dimension. It does
not impose additional Weyl--Heisenberg or order-three symmetry.

These frames attain the optimal coherence for \(d^2\) unit vectors and
lead to highly symmetric Gram matrices. Their construction is a concrete
structured matrix design and conditioning problem with applications to
state reconstruction. The existence theorem applies to every dimension
and yields exact overlap and tightness identities.

\subsection{Related partial result ---
2026-11-26}\label{related-partial-result-2026-11-26}

Hassan Khodaiemehr's November preprint reduces existence of a SIC in a
given dimension to a condition on the Galois conjugates of the overlap
units of an associated ghost SIC. Section VII identifies the unresolved
arithmetic hypotheses in the reduction. The exact checks in small
dimensions and the conditional implication do not supply the
unconditional arithmetic input established in the resolution above.
\href{https://arxiv.org/html/2611.01524v1}{Preprint, §§VI.4 and VII}.

\textbf{Tightness.} Let \(S=\sum_i\phi_i\phi_i^*\) for the formalized
witness. Then \(\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits S=d^2\) and

\[\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits S^2
=\sum_{i,j}|\phi_i^*\phi_j|^2
=d^2+\frac{d^2(d^2-1)}{d+1}=d^3.\]

Hence
\(\|S-dI_d\|_F^2=\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits S^2-2d\mathop{\mathrm{tr}}\nolimits S+d^3=0\).
Thus the same vectors satisfy both displays in the canonical statement.
The trace argument is the catalog's mathematical statement comparison.
