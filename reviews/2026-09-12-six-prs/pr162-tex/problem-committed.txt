Problem statement
Fix an integer 𝑠 ≥ 2 and 𝐶 ≥ 1. Suppose 𝑟𝑘 ≤ 𝑘, 𝑘/𝑟𝑘 → 𝐶, and 𝑛𝑘 /𝑟𝑘 → ∞. For every deterministic
sequence 𝑀𝑘 ∈ {−1, 0, 1}𝑘×𝑛𝑘 with exactly 𝑠 nonzero entries per column, and a uniformly random 𝑟𝑘 element column set 𝐼𝑘 , prove or disprove
∀𝜂 > 0,

Pr{𝜎min ((𝑀𝑘 )∶𝐼𝑘 ) > 𝜂} ⟶ 0.

Here 𝜎min (𝐵) = inf‖𝑥‖2 =1 ‖𝐵𝑥‖2 . There is no hypothesis controlling intersections of column supports.
