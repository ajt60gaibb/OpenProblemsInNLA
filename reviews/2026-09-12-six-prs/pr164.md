## Independent reconstruction of the proof

I read the complete original and attributed TeX manuscripts, independently checked the argument, and then compared the submitted review with my conclusions. The supplied PASS report and finite checks were not treated as proof certificates.

1. Fix any positive spectrum and any real orthogonal conjugate `K = V^T Lambda V`. Every `K_ii > 0`, so each one-column Nyström residual is defined. Symmetry gives `tr(K_:i K_i:) = sum_j K_ij^2 = (K^2)_ii`. Its error is therefore `E_i = S1 - (K^2)_ii / K_ii`, where `S1 = tr K` and `S2 = tr K^2`.
2. The positive weights `K_ii / S1` sum to one. Their weighted mean of the errors equals `S1 - S2/S1`, independent of `V`. Thus `min_i E_i <= S1-S2/S1` for every `V`, and maximizing over `V` preserves that upper bound. This checks the direction of the max-min quantifiers.
3. Every real symmetric trace-zero matrix has an orthonormal basis of zero quadratic-form vectors. For a nonzero such matrix there are eigenvalues `p > 0` and `-q < 0`. The vector `sqrt(q/(p+q)) u_+ + sqrt(p/(p+q)) u_-` has norm one and zero quadratic form. Compressing to its orthogonal complement leaves a symmetric matrix with zero trace, because the trace is the sum of this zero diagonal entry and the compression trace. Induction, with the zero and one-dimensional cases included, constructs the full basis. No invariant-subspace assumption is needed for the compression.
4. Apply that lemma to `H = Lambda^2 - c Lambda`, with `c = S2/S1`. This is a real symmetric trace-zero matrix. Put the zero-quadratic-form basis vectors in the columns of `V`. Orthogonality implies `K^2 = V^T Lambda^2 V`; hence `(K^2)_ii = c K_ii` for every column. Every error is exactly `S1-c`, so one single admissible orthogonal matrix attains the upper bound simultaneously for every subset in this slice.
5. The identity `2e2 = S1^2-S2` identifies this value with `y1 = 2e2/e1`. It is positive for the given strictly positive spectrum and `n >= 3`. Therefore `x1 = y1` for every spectrum and the ratio's supremum is exactly one. Uniform spectra cause no degeneracy: `H = 0` is explicitly included in the lemma. The exhibited matrix establishes attainment directly; compactness of `O(n)` and continuity also justify the maximum notation.
