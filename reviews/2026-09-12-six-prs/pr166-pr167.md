The non-diagonal proposed roots are positive multiples of rank-one outer products; the remaining roots are nonnegative diagonal matrices. Independent exact SymPy reconstruction checks their squares against all six Gram matrices.

The two symmetric moduli have the exact form I/8 + uu*/10 - E/8, with u=(3,1,0) or (3,0,1) and E the appropriate missing-coordinate projection. Their sum argument's modulus is diag(3/4,3/8,3/8). For any two complex unitaries U,V, rank-nullity for the complex-linear map w -> ((Uu)*w,(Vv)*w) supplies a nonzero common orthogonal vector. Its squared Euclidean length is proved positive. The rotated outer products vanish on this vector, while the subtracted projections contribute nonpositively. Hence the right quadratic form is at most sqrt(2)/4 times squared length and the left at least 3/8 times squared length. The strict scalar gap is exact; the kernel-mode LeanCert certificate 2<9/4 is consumed by the final contradiction. The quadratic-form monotonicity lemma uses Matrix.le_iff, i.e. PSD order, not entrywise order. No generic matrix diagonalization, sampled unitary or numerical square root is assumed.

The six public declarations prove every bridge and negate the full coefficient-sqrt(2) assertion without an additional premise. The source manuscript's stronger absence of any finite constant is correctly distinguished as outside these exports.
