# RA-05 hyperplane bridge: pre-proof exact support contract

This packet is a support component, not a proof of RA-05. The canonical status must remain Solved. It preserves the full unchanged RA-05 statement, including `Classification ∧ NegativeAnswer`, and imports exact published copies of RA05 and RA06 definitions. `SOURCE_LOCK.json` binds them to published main. Original IDs, canonical pages, targets, and preexisting dirty files are untouched.

The proposed three signatures in Challenge.lean are independently reviewable before implementation. They state: (1) for every positive dimension d and every real unit vector x, the matrix I−xxᵀ is a symmetric idempotent of rank at most d−1; (2) the actual RA05 residual row cost at this projector is the actual RA06 absolute row action raised to the same real p; (3) for every n,d with d>0, every real matrix A, p>0, every real accuracy ε, and the original nonnegative weights w, an actual strong coreset at rank d−1 preserves the actual all-vector lp energy at the same ε and w. No full-column-rank, input-rank, sampler, dimension bound, finite-certificate, or prescribed-weight assumption is added.

Proof argument: for unit x, P=I−xxᵀ is symmetric and satisfies P²=P. It kills the nonzero vector x, so rank P<d, hence rank P≤d−1. A row a has residual a−aP=(a·x)x; the sum of its squared coordinates equals (a·x)², and sqrt of that sum equals |a·x|. Apply StrongCoreset to this actual P and sum the exact row identities. For arbitrary nonzero y, normalize x=y/sqrt(∑y_j²); its denominator is strictly positive. Both the original and weighted energies scale by the same strictly positive factor (sqrt(∑y_j²))^p, so cancel that factor from the two inequalities. For y=0, p>0 makes all energies zero. The d=1 projector has rank zero and is included. No sign condition on 1−ε is needed, since only positive factors are canceled.

This bridge is needed to apply RA06's deterministic graph support obstruction to arbitrary strong coresets. It does not convert RA06's final sampler-specific negative answer into RA05's full negative answer. The graph obstruction and ε=k^(−a) yield a rate contradiction for 2<p<3 when (p−2)/(2(p−1))<a≤1/(p+1); the interval is nonempty exactly for 2<p<3. That limitation must remain explicit. For p≥3 the graph rate does not dominate the retained additive proposal. Full Classification additionally needs the source's non-even Walsh and even mixed-feature/partial-coloring arguments.

The mathematical documents are retained byte for byte under source/. Their informal reviews are evidence of the source mathematics, not Lean or Comparator evidence.
