## Mathematical proof checks

Both reviewers read the complete direct manuscript independently and found
no gap in these decisive steps:

1. Theorem 2.1, pp. 3–5: the exterior divided difference is nonzero on the
   reciprocal bidisk. Its logarithmic derivative has only strictly mixed
   negative Laurent powers, hence zero circle marginals. The convex support
   inequality gives a nonnegative boundary kernel.
2. Proposition 2.2, p. 5: the positive kernel with both marginals one is an
   `L²` contraction; Jensen/Schur-Markov and Parseval control the negative
   Faber coefficient component for arbitrary matrix coefficients.
3. Theorem 3.1, p. 6: the resolvent positivity follows by congruence from
   the numerical-range supporting-line inequality. Theorem 3.2, pp. 6–8,
   retains both multiplication orders `FG` and `GF`; dual coefficient bounds,
   Parseval and the positive `2×2` block trace inequality force the complete
   bound `γ ≤ 2`.
4. Lemma 4.1 and the final proof, pp. 9–10, shrink analytic convex outer
   domains to any numerical range, including a point or line segment.
   Appendix A, pp. 10–11, supplies the conformal exterior collar used in the
   smooth-domain argument.

This is an informal mathematical review, not external human peer review. It
does not audit the companion stronger Hilbert-space manuscript.
