Theorem 1.4, printed p. 2, gives an absolute `C` with `p_d ≤ C/d` for all

quantifiers and injectivity definition match the original FR-05 page.

## Proof checks

1. Section 3.1, pp. 4–5, defines the planted row density and a
   covariance-matched Gaussian reference density. Their normalization,
   covariance and right-multiplication representation yield the exact
   reference identity `P_r(E_d)=p_d` for the injectivity event.
2. Proposition 3.1, pp. 5 and 11–13, shows that an exact rank-two indefinite
   Hermitian kernel obstruction exists under the planted law outside an
   `O(d⁻²)` exceptional event. Lemmas 3.6–3.7 supply the small-ball and least
   singular value bounds. The quantitative Newton step makes the planted
   approximate obstruction exact; its chart yields inequivalent signals.
3. Proposition 3.2, pp. 5 and 6–11, bounds the squared likelihood difference
   by `O(d⁻¹)`. The audit checked the cone estimate (Lemma 3.3), the shared
   quadratic term and cancellation in the pairwise kernels (Lemma 3.4), the
   Haar two-frame overlap density (Lemma 3.5), and the local integral and tail
   split. The covariance match is essential to the stated order.
4. The reduction on p. 6 applies Cauchy–Schwarz to obtain
   `p_d ≤ a/d² + sqrt(b p_d/d)` for large `d`. Solving this inequality gives
   `p_d = O(d⁻¹)`; enlarging the constant covers the finite prefix.
